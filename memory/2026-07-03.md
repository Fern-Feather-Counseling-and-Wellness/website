**Text:** Setting Boundaries: Your Most Powerful Self-Care Tool as a Therapist.

**Slide 2:**
*Image: A visual representation of a healthy boundary – maybe a protective circle or a well-organized desk.*
**Text:** Why Boundaries Matter (More Than You Think!)
As therapists, we pour so much into our clients. Clear boundaries protect your energy, prevent burnout, and ensure you can show up fully, session after session.

**Slide 3:**
*Image: A split image – one side showing burnout (crumpled paper, tired eyes), the other showing well-being (calm therapist, healthy activity).*
**Text:** Burnout is Real. Boundaries Are Your Shield.
Untended boundaries can lead to emotional exhaustion, compassion fatigue, and decreased effectiveness. Healthy boundaries are non-negotiable for sustainable practice. (Source: e.g., Figley, 1995 for compassion fatigue)

**Slide 4:**
*Image: Icons representing different types of boundaries – phone, clock, calendar, personal space.*
**Text:** Types of Boundaries:
🚫 **Time:** Session limits, work hours, avoiding double-bookings.
📱 **Digital:** Managing client communication, response times.
↔️ **Emotional:** Not taking on client distress as your own, self-awareness.
👤 **Physical:** Space in your office, and personal space.

**Slide 5:**
*Image: A therapist smiling, engaging in a hobby, or spending time in nature.*
**Text:** Practical Tips:
1.  **Schedule It:** Block out non-negotiable self-care time in your calendar.
2.  **Communicate Clearly:** State your boundaries politely but firmly.
3.  **Say NO:** It’s okay to decline requests that overextend you.
4.  **Debrief & Reflect:** Process your own challenges with boundaries with a supervisor or peer.

**Slide 6:**
*Image: A strong, healthy tree with deep roots – symbolizing groundedness and resilience.*
**Text:** Investing in Boundaries = Investing in Your Clients.
When you honor your own needs, you're better equipped to support your clients' healing journeys. Your well-being is foundational.
