**Slide 2:** What is Epigenetics? 
   - Definition: chemical modifications that regulate gene expression without changing DNA sequence.
   - Key mechanisms: DNA methylation, histone modification, non‑coding RNAs.

**Slide 3:** How Trauma Impacts Epigenetics 
   - Chronic stress alters methylation patterns in stress‑regulation genes (e.g., NR3C1).
   - Evidence: McGowan et al., 2022; Szyf et al., 2021.

**Slide 4:** Intergenerational Transmission 
   - Parenting behaviors, prenatal environment, and post‑natal stress can pass epigenetic marks to offspring.
   - Real‑world example: Holocaust survivor studies (Yehuda et al., 2020).
