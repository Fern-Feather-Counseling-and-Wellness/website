# Competitor Analysis – CEU Courses for Therapists

**Objective:** Identify key competitors offering CEU‑credit courses for mental‑health clinicians, focusing on content, pricing, delivery format, and marketing positioning.

## Top Competitors (as of April 2026)
| Competitor | Course Focus | CEU Credit | Delivery Method | Price (USD) | Notable Features |
|------------|--------------|------------|-----------------|------------|-------------------|
| **PESI** | Trauma, CBT, Somatic | Up to 15 CEUs per course | Live webinars + on‑demand recordings | $150‑$350 per course | Strong brand, accredited by NBCC, large email list |
| **Zur Institute** | Various modalities (EMDR, DBT, Mind‑Body) | 1‑30 CEUs | Self‑paced video modules | $199‑$599 | Extensive marketing funnel, upsells to certification bundles |

| **Somatic Experiencing Trauma Institute** | Somatic interventions | 10‑15 CEUs | Hybrid (live + recordings) | $250‑$400 | Emphasizes body‑based science, strong community presence |
| **Brookline Institute (online arm)** | Evidence‑based practices | 5‑25 CEUs | Live online seminars | $175‑$375 | Offers continuing education bundles for multiple specialties |

## Common Strengths Across Competitors
- **Accreditation:** All are NBCC‑accredited, ensuring CEU eligibility.
- **Modular Format:** Courses broken into 30‑minute or 1‑hour modules for flexibility.
- **Marketing Assets:** High‑quality video production, strong testimonial use, email nurture sequences.
- **Pricing Tiers:** Tiered pricing based on number of CEUs, with bundle discounts.
- **Delivery Platforms:** Use proprietary LMS or platforms like Teachable, Kajabi.
