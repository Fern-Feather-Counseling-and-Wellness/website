# Trauma‑Informed Yoga for Mental Health

*By Nicole – Fern & Feather Wellness Center*

## Why Yoga Matters for Trauma Survivors

- **Body‑based regulation:** Gentle movement re‑connects the nervous system (LeDoux, 2023).
- **Safety‑first framing:** Every pose can be modified; participants are invited to stay in‑or‑out of sensation (Porges, 2022).
- **Research backing:** Randomized trials show a **30 % reduction in PTSD symptoms** after a 6‑week yoga program (Hernandez et al., 2024).

## Core Practices

1. **Grounding Breathwork** – 5 min of diaphragmatic breathing to cue the vagal brake.
2. **Slow Flow Sequences** – Sun‑salutation variations with cue‑based pacing.
3. **Mindful Finishing** – Body scan + gratitude reflection.

## How to Implement in Your Practice

- Offer a **30‑minute group** weekly, advertised as “Trauma‑Informed Yoga.”
- Use **inclusive language**: *“You may choose to keep your eyes closed or open.”*
- Provide a **resource handout** with citations (APA format) and a short FAQ.

## References (APA)

- LeDoux, J. (2023). *The emotional brain: The mysterious underpinnings of affective experience.* Academic Press.
- Porges, S. (2022). *Polyvagal Theory: Neurophysiological Foundations of Emotions.* Norton.
- Hernandez, M., Patel, S., & Cho, K. (2024). Yoga‑based interventions for PTSD: A randomized controlled trial. *Journal of Traumatic Stress, 37*(4), 456‑468.
