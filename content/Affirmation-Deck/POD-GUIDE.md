# How to Sell Your Affirmation Deck with Print-on-Demand (POD)

This guide walks you through getting your **52-card Fern's Feels deck** from concept to sale using a POD company, sold on your website.

---

## What is Print-on-Demand?

POD = You design it, customer buys it, POD company prints & ships it. **No inventory needed.**

You pay: Production cost + shipping  
Customer pays: Retail price (you set)  
Your profit: Retail - (production + shipping)

---

| Company | Best For | Card Deck Support | Notes |
|---------|----------|-------------------|-------|
| **Printify** | Price, variety | ✅ Yes | 80+ print providers; often cheaper |
| **Printful** | Quality, integration | ✅ Yes | Slightly higher quality, better integrations |

**The Game Crafter** or **MakePlayingCards** — they're built specifically for cards, so the quality is better than general POD services.

## Step 2: Prepare Your Artwork

For a 52-card deck, you need:

### Front of Each Card:
- Illustration (Fern or crew member)
- Phrase text

### Back of Each Card:
- THE TRUTH section
- REFLECT section  
- TINY STEP section

### File Requirements (varies by printer):
- **Resolution:** 300 DPI minimum
- **Format:** PDF (preferred) or high-res PNG/JPG
- **Color Mode:** CMYK (not RGB)
- **Bleed:** 0.125" - 0.25" extra around edges

### Card Size Options:
- **Standard poker size:** 2.5" x 3.5" (63.5mm x 89mm)
- **Tarot size:** 2.75" x 4.75" (70mm x 120mm)
- **Mini deck:** 1.75" x 2.5"

### What to Order First:
Start with a **sample/proof** — order 1-2 decks to check quality before listing publicly.

---

## Step 3: Upload to POD Platform

### On Printify/Printful:
1. Create account
2. "Add product" → choose "Notebook" or "Playing Cards" (if available)
3. Upload your front/back design
4. Set retail price
5. Connect to your store

### On The Game Crafter:
1. Create account
2. "Create New Project" → "Card Game"
3. Upload front and back images for each card
4. Choose paper stock, finish, box option
5. Set price or leave "call for pricing"

### On MakePlayingCards:
1. Upload template (they provide one)
2. Fill in front/back for each card
3. Choose quantity (can do as low as 1 for proof)
4. Select finish (matte recommended for writing on cards)

---

## Step 4: Connect to Your Website

### Option A: Direct Integration (Easiest)
- **Printify** connects directly to: Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace, BigCommerce, Etsy
- **Printful** connects to same plus: Squarespace, Wix, WooCommerce

**How it works:** Customer buys on your site → order auto-sends to POD → POD prints & ships → you get paid the difference

### Option B: Manual Orders
- List product on your site with "made to order" note
- When someone buys, you order from POD manually
- POD ships to customer
- Higher margin but more work

---

## Step 5: Pricing Your Deck

### Cost Example (Printify - playing cards):
- Production: ~$8-12 per deck
- Shipping to you: ~$5-8
- Shipping to customer: ~$4-6 (usually included or added)

### Retail Pricing Guide:
- **Cost to you:** $12-18 per deck
- **Your price:** $25-35 (target 40-50% margin)
- **Digital PDF:** $12-15 (higher margin, instant download)

### Bundle Ideas:
- Deck + Trauma Biology Workbook
- Deck + Journal
- Deck + mini meditation guide
