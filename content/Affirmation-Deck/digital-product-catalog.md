# Fern & Feather Digital Product Catalog

Welcome to the Fern & Feather Wellness Center product hub. Below is a curated list of digital offerings designed to support neurodivergent, trauma‑survivor, and anxiety‑prone individuals. Each product is built around Fern, the brave little crow who sits beside you while you heal.

---

## 1. **Fern's Feels – 52‑Card Self‑Care Deck**
* **Format:** Printable PDF & POD printed deck (standard 2.5" × 3.5").
* **What it offers:** 52 cards with Fern’s honest phrases, psycho‑educational “THE TRUTH,” reflective prompts, and actionable “TINY STEP.”
* **Why it matters:** A tangible reminder that bravery is showing up, even when scared.

---

## 2. **The Book of Fern**
* **Format:** PDF manuscript (≈ 40 pages).
* **Content:** Fern’s back‑story, core truths, the “Empty Chair Rule,” and the “Fern Filter.”
* **Purpose:** Provides the narrative voice for the deck and deepens the connection.

---

## 3. **Mini‑Course: Brave‑Practice Journaling**
* **Length:** 4 modules (15 min each).
* **Modules:**
  1. *Seeing the Mask* – Recognize protective habits.
  2. *The Tiny Truth* – Turn insights into daily actions.
  3. *Presence Over Advice* – Practicing silent support.
  4. *Self‑Compassion Check‑Ins* – Simple daily rituals.
* **Outcome:** Learners build a habit of showing up for themselves, just like Fern.
