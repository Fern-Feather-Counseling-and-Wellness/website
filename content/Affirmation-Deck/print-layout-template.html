🎴 Fern's Feels — Print Layout Guide

How to Use This Template:
1. Replace each [ILLUSTRATION] placeholder with artwork
2. Front and back are laid out for double-sided printing — flip on SHORT edge
3. Print on 13"x19" cardstock, or use 8.5"x11" (2 cards per page, landscape)
4. Cut along crop marks, round corners if desired

Card Specifications

  • Card Size: 3.5" x 2.5" (poker card standard)
  • Print Size: 3.125" x 4.375" (with bleed/crop marks)
  • Paper: 12-14pt cardstock, matte
  • Finishing: Round corners (optional), UV coating (optional)

Front Design Elements

  • Dark background (#1a1a1a) for contrast with light text
  • Fern/crew illustration centered (2" x 2")
  • Phrase in bold, white text, centered below illustration
  • Small Fern & Feather logo or "Fern's Feels" text at bottom

Back Design Elements

  • Light background (#fafafa)
  • THE TRUTH: Dark green accent (#2d5a4a)
  • REFLECT: Warm brown accent (#5a4a2d)
  • TINY STEP: Purple accent (#4a2d5a)
