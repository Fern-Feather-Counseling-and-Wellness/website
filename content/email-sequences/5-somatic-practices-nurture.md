# 3‑Email Nurture Sequence: 5 Somatic Practices Lead Magnet

## Audience
- New subscribers who downloaded the "5 Somatic Practices for Stress Relief" PDF.
- Primarily therapists, yoga instructors, and wellness‑focused professionals.

## Goal
- Provide additional value, build trust, and gently introduce Fern & Feather’s paid offerings (e.g., printable toolkits, membership).
- Encourage engagement (click‑throughs, replies, social shares).

---

## Email 1 – Welcome & Quick Wins (Sent immediately after download)
**Subject:** Welcome! Here’s your first somatic boost 🚀

**Body:**
- Thank them for downloading the guide.
- Brief recap of the 5 practices (one‑sentence each).
- **Quick Win:** Highlight one practice (e.g., 4‑7‑8 breath) with a short “try it now” instruction.
- CTA: Invite them to reply with their favorite practice.
- Footer: Link to the full blog post on “Integrating Yoga into Trauma‑Informed Therapy”.

**Key Elements:**
- Friendly, concise tone (sharp but warm).
- Include a visual (link to Instagram carousel on HRV basics).
- Social icons for Instagram/TikTok.

---

## Email 2 – Deep Dive & Social Proof (Sent 2 days later)
**Subject:** How these five practices transform therapist life 🌿

**Body:**
- Share a short case study of a therapist who reduced burnout using the 5 practices (anonymized).
- Provide deeper explanations for two practices (e.g., grounding 5‑4‑3‑2‑1 and micro‑body scan) with research citations.
- **Bonus Resource:** Link to a 2‑minute TikTok “Somatic Reset for Therapists”.
- CTA: Invite them to join the upcoming Mindful Listening webinar (link).
- Footer: Reminder of the lead‑magnet PDF download link.

---

## Email 3 – Offer & Community Invite (Sent 5 days after download)
**Subject:** Ready for the next level? Unlock our Printable Toolkit 🎁

**Body:**
- Recap the benefits they’ve experienced from the guide.
- Introduce the **Fern & Feather Printable Toolkit** (price, contents, how it expands the 5 practices).
- Offer a **20 % discount code** (e.g., “THERAPIST20”) valid for 48 hours.
- Social proof: testimonials from two therapists.
- CTA: “Grab your toolkit now” button linking to the product page.
- Secondary CTA: Join our private Facebook group for ongoing support.

---

## Tracking & Metrics
- **Open Rates:** Aim > 45 % for Email 1, > 35 % for Email 2, > 30 % for Email 3.
- **Click‑Through Rates:** ≥ 12 % (links to blog, TikTok, product).
- **Conversion:** At least 5 % of sequence recipients use the discount code.
- **Replies:** Monitor reply rate for engagement insights.
