# Module 7 – Navigating School & Social Settings

**Learning Objectives**
- Understand common challenges neurodivergent children face in school environments.
- Equip parents with evidence‑based strategies to advocate for accommodations.
- Provide tools for fostering peer relationships and social confidence.

---

## 1. Common School‑Related Challenges
- Sensory overload in noisy classrooms (Ashburner et al., 2021).
- Executive‑function difficulties affecting organization and time‑management (Willcutt, 2020).
- Social misunderstanding and bullying prevalence (Huang & Zhou, 2022).

## 2. Advocacy & Accommodation Checklist
| Need | Example Accommodation | How to Request |
|------|----------------------|----------------|
| Sensory | Seat away from windows, noise‑cancelling headphones | Write a formal request to the school’s 504 coordinator, cite relevant research (e.g., Ashburner 2021). |
| Executive Function | Planner templates, extended time on assignments | Meet with the special‑education teacher, propose a pilot trial.
| Social | Structured peer‑buddy system | Coordinate with school counselor, suggest a weekly “buddy check‑in”. |

## 3. Building Social Skills at Home
- **Play‑Based Role‑Playing** – practice greeting, turn‑taking, and body‑language cues.
- **Interest‑Based Clubs** – leverage child’s passions (e.g., robotics, art) to find like‑minded peers.
- **Mindful Breathing Before Social Interactions** – 4‑7‑8 technique to reduce anxiety.
