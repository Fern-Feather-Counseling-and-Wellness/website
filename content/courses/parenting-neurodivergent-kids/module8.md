# Module 8 – Supporting Transition to Adulthood

**Learning Objectives**
- Identify challenges neurodivergent youth face when moving into adult roles (higher education, employment, independent living).
- Provide evidence‑based strategies for parents to support autonomy while maintaining appropriate scaffolding.
- Equip families with resources for legal rights, accommodations, and community support.

---

## 1. Common Transition Challenges
- **Executive‑function demands**: Managing finances, scheduling, and self‑organization (Willcutt, 2020).
- **Social navigation**: Forming adult friendships, workplace dynamics (Huang & Zhou, 2022).
- **Sensory environment shifts**: New living spaces, workplace noise (Ashburner et al., 2021).

## 2. Practical Support Strategies
### a. Structured Planning Tools
| Tool | Purpose | Example |
|------|---------|---------|
| Digital Calendar with reminders | Automate appointments, bill payments | Google Calendar with weekly “pay rent” reminder |
| Weekly Review Worksheet | Reflect on successes & obstacles | Fill out every Sunday: tasks completed, challenges, next‑week plan |
| Visual Schedules | Provide clear daily routine | Printable poster with icons for morning, study, breaks, evening |

### b. Gradual Skill Building
- **Financial Literacy**: Start with budgeting apps (YNAB, Mint); practice with mock budgets before real accounts.
- **Self‑Advocacy Role‑Play**: Simulate workplace conversations about accommodations (e.g., flexible hours, noise‑cancelling headphones).
- **Housing Checklists**: Evaluate potential living spaces for sensory-friendly features (lighting, soundproofing).

### c. Community and Legal Resources
- **Independent Living Programs** – Local agencies offering step‑by‑step training.
- **ADA & Section 504** – Overview of rights for post‑secondary education and employment.
- **Support Groups** – Adult neurodivergent meet‑ups (e.g., ADDA, Autism Society chapters).

---

## 3. Emotional Support for Parents
- **Self‑Compassion Practices** – Short guided meditations to reduce parental guilt (Kabat‑Zinn, 2023).
- **Counseling Referral** – Encourage parents to seek therapy focused on transition stress.
- **Peer Mentor Networks** – Connect with other parents who have navigated similar transitions.

---

---

## 5. Implementation Checklist (for families)
1. ✅ Set up a digital calendar with recurring reminders for key adult tasks (bills, appointments).
2. ✅ Complete a weekly review worksheet for the first month.
3. ✅ Practice a self‑advocacy script with a trusted friend or mentor.
4. ✅ Identify at least one community resource (support group, independent living program).
5. ✅ Schedule a brief counseling session to discuss transition stress.
