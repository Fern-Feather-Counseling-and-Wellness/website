# Instagram Carousel: 5 Grounding Techniques

## Slide 1 – Introduction
**Feeling overwhelmed?** Grounding brings you back to the present moment, calming the nervous system. Swipe for five simple practices you can try anywhere.

## Slide 2 – 5‑4‑3‑2‑1 Sensory Exercise
**Look around and name:**
- 5 things you can see
- 4 things you can touch
- 3 things you can hear
- 2 things you can smell
- 1 thing you can taste

A quick way to anchor your mind.

## Slide 3 – Breath Box (4‑4‑4‑4)
**Inhale** for 4 counts, **hold** for 4, **exhale** for 4, **hold** again for 4. Repeat three times. This rhythmic breathing steadies the heart rate.

## Slide 4 – Body Scan Mini‑Check‑In
**Mentally scan** from head to toe, noticing tension. Gently soften any tight spots. Even a 30‑second scan reduces stress hormones.

## Slide 5 – Grounding Object
Carry a small stone, crystal, or textured bead. When anxiety spikes, hold it, notice its weight, temperature, and texture. It creates a tactile anchor.

## Slide 6 – Quick Movement Burst
**Stand, shake out** your arms, roll your shoulders, and step in place for 15 seconds. Movement releases built‑up muscular tension and refocuses attention.
