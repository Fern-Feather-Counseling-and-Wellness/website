# Instagram Carousel: 5 Steps to Reduce Anxiety

**Slide 1 – Title**
"5 Simple Steps to Calm Your Mind"

**Slide 2 – Breathe Deeply**
📌 *Box breathing*: Inhale 4s → Hold 4s → Exhale 4s → Hold 4s. Repeat 4‑5 cycles.

**Slide 3 – Ground Yourself**
📌 *5‑4‑3‑2‑1 technique*: Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

**Slide 4 – Move Your Body**
📌 Quick stretch: Neck rolls, shoulder shrugs, spine twists. Even a 2‑minute walk resets the nervous system.

**Slide 5 – Re‑frame Thoughts**
📌 Ask: *Is this thought 100% true?* Challenge it, replace with a balanced statement.

**Slide 6 – Schedule a Reset**
📌 Set a daily "stress‑check" reminder on your phone. Consistency builds resilience.
