# Script 005: Why "Just Talk to Someone" Actually Works

**Hook (5 sec):** "Therapy isn't just talking. It's co-regulation."

**Body (45 sec):**
You've heard "talk to a therapist." But why does it actually WORK?

It's not just insight. It's not just being heard. It's **co-regulation.**

When you're in session with a regulated, attuned therapist, your nervous system literally learns to down-regulate. Their calm becomes your calm. Your vagus nerve picks up on their cues.

That's why the therapeutic relationship is the #1 predictor of outcomes — more than the modality (CBT, IFS, EMDR, etc.).

You're not just getting advice. You're getting a nervous system upgrade.
