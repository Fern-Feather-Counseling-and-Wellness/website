# TikTok Script – Managing Client Boundaries

**Hook (0‑5 s):**
"Ever feel like your clients are pulling you into their drama 24/7?"

**Body (5‑25 s):**
- Quick definition: *Boundaries are the invisible lines that protect your time, energy, and wellbeing.*
- One‑minute tip: *When a client asks for extra sessions, reply with a firm, friendly schedule: “I can fit you in next week, but let’s keep our sessions to the agreed time so we both get the most out of them.”*
- Emphasize the why: healthy boundaries prevent burnout and keep therapy effective.

**Close (25‑30 s):**
"Set your limits, protect your space, and watch your practice thrive. Follow for more therapist‑friendly hacks!"
