# Script 002: The "Window of Tolerance" Explained

**Hook (5 sec):** "There's a reason you explode at small things — it's NOT about the small thing."

**Body (45 sec):**
Dr. Dan Siegel coined this thing called the **Window of Tolerance** — it's the zone where you're able to feel emotions without being overwhelmed.

When you're IN your window: you can handle stress, regulate emotions, stay present.

When you're OUTSIDE your window — either hyper-aroused (anxious, angry, panicky) OR hypo-aroused (numb, detached, shutdown) — you lose it. Small triggers cause big explosions.

The goal isn't to stay in your window 24/7. It's to **expand your window** through co-regulation, somatic work, and safe relationships.

Know your window. Expand it.
