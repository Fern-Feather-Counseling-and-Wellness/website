## How to Use This Library

1. **Pick your category** (Educational, Therapist Self-Care, or Client-Facing)
2. **Each script includes:**
   - Hook (first 3 seconds — critical for retention)
   - Body script (25-27 seconds)
   - Call-to-Action (final 3 seconds)
   - Visual suggestions
   - Hashtag recommendations

3. **Filming tips:**
   - Hook must grab attention in first 3 seconds
   - Look at the camera like you're talking to one person
   - Use captions (80% watch without sound)
   - Keep energy engaging but authentic
