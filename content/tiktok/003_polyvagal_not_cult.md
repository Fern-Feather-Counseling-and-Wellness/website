# Script 003: Polyvagal Theory — Not a Cult

**Hook (5 sec):** "Polyvagal theory gets a lot of hate. Here's what's actually valid."

**Body (45 sec):**
Stephen Porges' Polyvagal Theory says your vagus nerve — the wandering nerve — controls your relaxation response. It's divided into two branches: ventral (social engagement) and dorsal (shutdown/collapse).

Critics say it's oversimplified. They're not wrong — it's a clinical heuristic, not a perfect biological map.

But here's what's useful: **the hierarchy.** Your system prioritizes connection first, then mobilization, then shutdown. When we can't co-regulate, we dorsal vagal — we dissociate, go numb, freeze.

The science may be debated. The experience? Real. Your nervous system knows the truth even if textbooks haven't caught up.
