# Script 001: Why "Fight or Flight" Gets Stuck

**Hook (5 sec):** "Your nervous system is not broken — it's just trying to protect you."

**Body (45 sec):**
Ever feel like you're constantly on edge? Like your body is bracing for impact even when there's no threat? That's your sympathetic nervous system doing exactly what it's designed to do — keep you safe.

The problem? Sometimes it gets stuck. Chronic stress, trauma, or just years of "push through" can keep your fight-or-flight response activated way too long.

The fix isn't to "calm down." It's to **co-regulate** — with breath, movement, or a safe person.

Your nervous system is not the enemy. It's been protecting you. Let's learn to negotiate with it.
