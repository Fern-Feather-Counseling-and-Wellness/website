# TikTok Script: Trauma‑Informed Yoga

**Hook (0‑5 s)**
- "Ever wonder why yoga *feels* different after trauma? Let’s break down the science in 45 seconds."

**Body (5‑35 s)**
1. **Safety first** – The nervous system needs a sense of safety before any movement helps (Porges, 2017).  
2. **Grounding breath** – 4‑2‑4 breathing (inhale 4 sec, hold 2 sec, exhale 4 sec) activates the ventral vagal complex, lowering cortisol (Thoma et al., 2020).  
3. **Micro‑movements** – Slow, joint‑by‑joint transitions keep the body in the *window of tolerance* (van der Kolk, 2015).  
4. **Body scan** – Ending with a 30‑second body‑scan re‑engages interoception, essential for trauma integration.

**CTA (35‑45 s)**
- "Try this 4‑step practice today and notice a calmer mind. Follow for more science‑backed yoga tips!"
