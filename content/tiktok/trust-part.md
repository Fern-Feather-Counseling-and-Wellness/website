# TikTok Script – Building Trust with the PART Framework (30‑sec)

**Hook (0‑5s):**
"Ever wonder why some relationships feel *instant* and safe, while others feel like walking on eggshells?" 

**Brief intro (5‑10s):**
"It’s all about the brain’s *social‑engagement system* and a simple 4‑step recipe I call **PART**." 

**Step 1 – Presence (10‑15s):**
"First, *show up* fully. Put the phone away, make eye contact. Your nervous system signals safety only when you’re truly present." 

**Step 2 – Attunement (15‑20s):**
"Next, *tune in* to their inner world. Mirror their language, notice subtle cues. You’re basically saying ‘I see you.’" 

**Step 3 – Resonance (20‑25s):**
"Then, *match their vibe* – the tone, pace, emotion. This creates a shared rhythm, like two musicians syncing up." 

**Step 4 – Trust (25‑30s):**
"When presence, attunement, and resonance are in place, the brain flips into the ventral‑vagal “trust” mode. Suddenly you both feel safe, and the connection deepens." 

**CTA (30‑33s):**
"Try these 4 steps today in a conversation and notice the shift. Follow for more neuroscience‑based relationship hacks!"
