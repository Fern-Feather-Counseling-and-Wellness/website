# Competitor Analysis – Fern & Feather Wellness Center (2026)

**Date:** April 22, 2026

---

## Overview

To refine Fern & Feather’s positioning, pricing, and marketing strategy, I conducted a rapid competitor analysis of three leading therapy‑and‑wellness providers that blend neuroscience‑based counseling with somatic practices.

---

## 1. **CalmMind Therapy Collective**
- **Website:** https://calmmindtherapy.com
- **Core Offerings:**
  - Individual therapy (30‑45 min) – $150/hr
  - Group trauma‑informed yoga (90 min) – $55 per person
  - 4‑week online course “Neuro‑Body Integration” – $399
- **Target Audience:** Mid‑career professionals seeking evidence‑based somatic integration.
- **Strengths:**
  - Strong SEO ranking for “trauma‑informed yoga”.
  - Active Instagram presence (12k followers) with carousel posts.
  - Offers a free 15‑minute intake questionnaire.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - No LGBTQ+ specific branding.
  - Pricing is higher than average for group sessions.

---

## 2. **Rooted Healing Center**
- **Website:** https://rootedhealing.org
- **Core Offerings:**
  - Individual counseling (45 min) – $130/hr
  - Couples therapy – $180/hr
  - Monthly membership “Somatic Sanctuary” – $79/month (includes 2 group sessions & digital resources)
- **Target Audience:** Couples and families looking for holistic healing.
- **Strengths:**
  - Clear membership model creates recurring revenue.
  - Offers a downloadable “Self‑Compassion Toolkit”.
  - Strong community on Facebook (8k members).
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Limited blog content; SEO weak.
  - No TikTok presence.

---

## 3. **NeuroVibe Wellness**
- **Website:** https://neurovibewellness.com
- **Core Offerings:**
  - Individual therapy (60 min) – $165/hr
  - “Neuro‑Yoga” workshops (2‑hour) – $75 per person
  - Digital product “Brain‑Body Reset Pack” – $49
- **Target Audience:** Tech‑savvy professionals interested in neuroscience.
- **Strengths:**
  - Heavy use of data‑driven marketing (Google Ads, LinkedIn).
  - Publishes research‑summaries in their blog (high authority backlinks).
  - Offers a free “HRV Tracker” app integration.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Branding feels clinical, less personal.
  - Limited focus on LGBTQ+ inclusivity.

---

## Key Takeaways for Fern & Feather
1. **Pricing Strategy** – Competitors charge $130‑$165 for 45‑60 min individual sessions. Fern & Feather could position at $140‑$150 to stay competitive while emphasizing science‑based approach.
2. **Membership Model** – Consider a low‑cost monthly membership ($69) that includes 1 group yoga session + access to digital resources (e.g., somatic toolkit). This can generate recurring revenue and increase client retention.
3. **Content Channels** – TikTok is under‑utilized among competitors (except NeuroVibe). Doubling down on short‑form video (daily hooks) can give Fern & Feather a distinct edge.
4. **LGBTQ+ Branding** – Highlighting LGBTQ+ affirming language differentiates from CalmMind and NeuroVibe, aligning with Nicole’s mission.
5. **Lead Magnet Upsell** – Transform the existing 5‑somatic‑practices lead magnet into a gated email funnel that feeds into the membership model.
