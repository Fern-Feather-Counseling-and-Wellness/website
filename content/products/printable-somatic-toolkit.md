# Fern & Feather Printable Somatic Toolkit

## Overview

The **Printable Somatic Toolkit** provides a collection of somatic exercises, mindfulness prompts, and therapist guides designed to help clients regulate their nervous system. It complements the main Printable Toolkit with a deeper focus on movement‑based regulation.

## Contents

1. **Somatic Exercise Deck** – 12 printable cards with step‑by‑step instructions for grounding, body scans, and gentle movement sequences.
2. **Heartbeat Tracker** – Simple worksheet for clients to log physiological cues (heart‑rate, breathing patterns) before and after exercises.
3. **Facilitator Quick‑Guide** – One‑page cheat sheet for therapists to introduce each exercise, note contraindications, and suggest modifications.
4. **Client Handout: The Science of Somatic Regulation** – Brief overview of the autonomic nervous system, vagus nerve, and how somatic practices restore balance (with APA citations).
5. **Safety Checklist** – Protocol for ensuring a trauma‑informed environment (privacy, consent, trigger warnings).
