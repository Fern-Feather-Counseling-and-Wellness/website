# Fern & Feather Printable Toolkit

## Overview

The **Fern & Feather Printable Toolkit** is a downloadable PDF package designed for therapists and wellness practitioners who want a ready‑to‑use set of somatic exercises, handouts, and session templates. It aligns with the science‑based, trauma‑informed approach of Fern & Feather and can be used in individual or group settings.

## What’s Inside

1. **Session Planner** – One‑page template to map out session goals, exercises, and timing.
2. **Somatic Exercise Cards** – 10 printable cards with brief instructions, cues, and safety notes (e.g., grounding breath, body scan, progressive muscle relaxation).
3. **Client Handouts**
   - *Understanding Your Nervous System* – plain‑language overview of ANS, vagus nerve, and trauma response.
   - *Self‑Regulation Toolkit* – daily practice sheet with space for tracking breath work, movement, and reflections.
4. **Safety & Consent Forms** – customizable PDFs for informed consent, trauma disclosure preferences, and emergency contact.
5. **Facilitator Guide** – Quick‑reference guide for clinicians on how to introduce each exercise, modify for different abilities, and integrate with talk therapy.
