# Instagram Carousel: HRV Basics for Therapists

**Slide 1 – Title**
*Why Heart‑Rate Variability (HRV) matters for you* 
- Hook: "Want a quick health check for your nervous system? Look at your HRV."

**Slide 2 – What is HRV?**
- HRV = variation in time between heartbeats.
- High HRV = flexible nervous system, better stress handling.
- Low HRV = rigid response, higher burnout risk.

**Slide 3 – Why Therapists Care**
- Your work spikes the amygdala → sympathetic activation.
- Tracking HRV shows how well you’re recovering between clients.

**Slide 4 – Simple Ways to Boost HRV**
1. **4‑7‑8 Breath** – 2 min before each session.
2. **Micro‑Yoga** – 5‑min gentle flow during breaks.
3. **Movement** – Walk or stretch for 3 min every hour.
4. **Cold Splash** – Splash face with cool water after intense calls.

**Slide 5 – Quick HRV Check**
- Use a wearable (Oura, Whoop, Apple Watch) – log resting HRV each morning.
- Aim for a gradual upward trend.

**Slide 6 – Call‑to‑Action**
- Try a 4‑7‑8 breath right now and notice the calm.
