# Instagram Carousel: Therapist Burnout Prevention\n\n**Slide 1 – Title**\n*Stop burnout before it starts*\n- Hook: \"Therapists, feeling exhausted? Here’s a quick reset.\"\n\n**Slide 2 – Recognize the Signs**\n- Persistent fatigue, irritability, loss of empathy.\n- Elevated heart‑rate variability (HRV) drop indicates stress.\n\n**Slide 3 – 4‑7‑8 Breath**\n- Inhale 4 s, hold 7 s, exhale 8 s.\n- Boosts vagal tone, raises HRV (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021).\n\n**Slide 4 – Micro‑Body Scan (30 s)**\n- Start at toes, notice sensations, move upward.\n- Lowers cortisol (Brown & Gerbarg, 2020).\n\n**Slide 5 – Grounding 5‑4‑3‑2‑1**\n- 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.\n- Shifts attention, calms amygdala.\n\n**Slide 6 – Quick Stretch**\n- Shoulder rolls, neck stretch, chest opener for 1 min.\n- Releases tension, activates parasympathetic system.\n\n**Slide 7 – Call‑to‑Action**\n- Try one technique now, notice the shift.\n- Share your favorite with #TherapistBurnout and tag @FernFeatherWellness.\n\n---\n\n*All tips are evidence‑based (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021; Brown & Gerbarg, 2020).*
