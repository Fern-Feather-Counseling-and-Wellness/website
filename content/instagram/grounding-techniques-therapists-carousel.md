# Instagram Carousel: Grounding Techniques for Therapists

**Slide 1 – Title**
*When the day feels overwhelming, ground yourself in 30 seconds.*
- Hook: "Therapists, need a reset? Try these quick grounding moves."

**Slide 2 – 5‑4‑3‑2‑1 Senses**
1. **5** things you can see – name them.
2. **4** things you can touch – feel them.
3. **3** sounds you hear.
4. **2** smells you detect.
5. **1** taste – sip water.
- **Why**: Shifts attention to present, reduces amygdala activation (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021).

**Slide 3 – Breath Box (4‑4‑4‑4)**
- Inhale 4 s, hold 4 s, exhale 4 s, hold 4 s. Repeat 3‑5 cycles.
- **Science**: Boosts vagal tone, raises HRV (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021).

**Slide 4 – Shoulder Roll & Stretch**
- Roll shoulders forward 5 times, then backward 5 times.
- Follow with a gentle neck stretch (ear to shoulder).
- **Benefit**: Releases muscular tension, activates parasympathetic pathways (Brown & Gerbarg, 2020).

**Slide 5 – Foot Grounding**
- Plant both feet firmly, feel the floor.
- Slightly press down, imagine roots grounding you.
- **Effect**: Physical grounding signals safety to the nervous system.

**Slide 6 – Quick Body Scan (30 s)**
- Starting at the toes, notice any sensations, move upward.
- No judgment, just observation.
- **Outcome**: Enhances interoceptive awareness, lowers cortisol (Brown & Gerbarg, 2020).
