# Instagram Carousel: Stress Management for Therapists

**Slide 1 – Title**
*Why therapists burn out (and how to prevent it)*
- Hook: "Your brain works overtime for your clients. Time to recharge yours!"

**Slide 2 – The Science**
- Chronic stress keeps the HPA axis activated, flooding cortisol (McEwen, 2020).
- Elevated cortisol impairs the pre‑frontal cortex, reducing empathy and decision‑making.

**Slide 3 – Quick Breath Reset**
- *4‑7‑8 breathing*: Inhale 4 s, hold 7 s, exhale 8 s.
- Boosts HRV, calms the amygdala (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021).

**Slide 4 – Micro‑Yoga Break**
- 5‑minute seated flow: Cat‑Cow, seated forward fold, gentle twists.
- Activates vagal tone, reduces cortisol (Brown & Gerbarg, 2020).

**Slide 5 – Digital Sabbatical**
- 30 min screen‑free zone before/after client sessions.
- Improves sleep, lowers sympathetic arousal (Harvard Health, 2022).

**Slide 6 – Call‑to‑Action**
- Try one breath reset before your next session and notice the shift.
