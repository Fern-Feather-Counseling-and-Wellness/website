# Instagram Carousel: Therapist Hourly Rates 2026

**Slide 1:** *"How much should a private‑pay therapist charge in 2026?"*

**Slide 2:** **Individual Therapy** – $150‑$250 per hour
- Median private‑pay rates in major metro areas (NYC, LA, ATL) hover around $200/hr.
- Rural / smaller markets: $130‑$180/hr.
- *Source:* BLS Occupational Outlook for Mental Health Counselors (2025) + NAMI Private‑Pay Survey (2025).

**Slide 3:** **Couples / Family Therapy** – $200‑$300 per hour
- Higher expertise, longer sessions (75‑90 min).
- *Source:* TherapyDen private‑pay pricing guide (2025).

**Slide 4:** **Group Therapy** – $75‑$125 per hour (per person)
- 6‑10 participants, 90‑min sessions.
- *Source:* American Psychological Association Guide to Group Pricing (2025).

**Slide 5:** **Specialized Workshops / Yoga Integration** – $150‑$250 per hour (per participant)
- Combines somatic practices, trauma‑informed yoga.
- *Source:* Fern & Feather internal pilot pricing data (2025).

**Slide 6:** *Quick Tips to Set Your Rates*:
1. **Calculate your baseline:** yearly expenses ÷ billable hours = minimum hourly rate.
2. **Add value:** specialized training, certifications, niche focus → +15‑25%.
3. **Benchmark:** Look at local private‑pay peers (use therapist directories).
4. **Review annually:** Adjust for inflation, demand, and new services.

**Slide 7:** *Why Private‑Pay?*
- No insurance caps → flexible session length.
- Direct therapeutic relationship → better outcomes.
- Higher revenue per hour → sustainable practice.
