# Instagram Carousel: Trauma‑Informed Yoga

---

**Slide 1:**
*Title:* "Yoga for Healing: Trauma‑Informed Edition"
*Hook:* "Your mat can be a safe space. Let’s make it count."

---

**Slide 2:**
*Caption:* "Safety First"
- Emphasize a grounded, non‑judgmental environment.
- Invite clients to set intention: "I am here to feel safe."

---

**Slide 3:**
*Caption:* "Gentle Breath Work"
- 4‑4‑6 inhale‑hold‑exhale pattern.
- Connect breath to the spine, anchoring the nervous system.

---

**Slide 4:**
*Caption:* "Slow, Supported Flow"
- Use props (blocks, blankets) for stability.
- Moves like Cat‑Cow, Child’s Pose, and seated forward folds, staying within comfort.

---

**Slide 5:**
*Caption:* "Mindful Movement"
- Focus on internal sensations, not on form.
- Encourage noticing tension and releasing it gently.

---

**Slide 6:**
*Caption:* "Grounding Finale"
- End with Savasana using a bolster under knees.
- 3‑minute body scan, reinforcing a sense of safety.
