**Slide 1:**
*Title:* "Ground Yourself, Guide Your Clients"
*Hook:* "When the world spins, you need a steady anchor."

---

**Slide 2:**
*Caption:* "5‑Second Breath Reset"
- Inhale for 4 seconds, hold 2, exhale 6. Repeat twice.
- Helps reset nervous system during high‑stress moments.

---

**Slide 3:**
*Caption:* "5‑Sense Scan"
- Notice 1 thing you see, 1 you hear, 1 you feel, 1 you smell, 1 you taste.
- Anchors client to the present, interrupts rumination.

---

**Slide 4:**
*Caption:* "Box Breathing (4‑4‑4‑4)"
- Inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4.
- Easy to teach, reduces sympathetic activation.

---

**Slide 5:**
*Caption:* "Self‑Touch Grounding"
- Press fingertips together, feel the pressure.
- Physical sensation gives instant sense of safety.

---

**Slide 6:**
*Caption:* "Micro‑Movement Breaks"
- Stand, stretch arms overhead, roll shoulders.
- Moves stored tension, boosts proprioceptive input.
