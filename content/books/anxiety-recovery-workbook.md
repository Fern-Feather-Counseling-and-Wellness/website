# Anxiety Recovery Workbook (Client‑Facing)

*Keywords: anxiety, recovery, CBT, somatic regulation, coping skills, therapist guide*  

---

## Introduction

Welcome to the **Anxiety Recovery Workbook**. This guide is designed for clients who are ready to actively work on reducing anxiety symptoms using a blend of evidence‑based cognitive‑behavioral strategies, somatic regulation techniques, and mindfulness practices.

---

## How to Use This Workbook

1. **Read** each chapter and complete the accompanying exercises.
2. **Track** your progress in the daily logs.
3. **Discuss** insights with your therapist during sessions.
4. **Revisit** sections as needed – anxiety recovery is a non‑linear journey.

---

## Chapter Outline

### 1. Understanding Anxiety
- Neurobiology of the stress response (amygdala, HPA axis)
- Common triggers and how they manifest (cognitive, physiological, behavioral)
- APA‑cited research on prevalence and impact (Kessler et al., 2021)

### 2. Grounding & Vagal Activation
- Diaphragmatic breathing (4‑7‑8 technique)
- Body scan meditation
- Simple movements to boost vagal tone (e.g., gentle sway, neck rolls)

### 3. Cognitive Restructuring
- Identifying automatic thoughts
- Thought‑record worksheet (ABC model)
- Challenging distortions with evidence‑based questions

### 4. Exposure & Response Prevention
- Creating a hierarchy of feared situations
- Gradual exposure planning
- Managing safety behaviors

### 5. Lifestyle & Self‑Care Strategies
- Sleep hygiene checklist
- Physical activity recommendations (aerobic & yoga)
- Nutrition basics for stress reduction

### 6. Relapse Prevention
- Early warning signs checklist
- Coping plan template
- Building a support network
