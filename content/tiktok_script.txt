# TikTok Script – 30-second Hook

**Hook (first 3 seconds):**
"Ever wonder why you feel stuck after a stressful day?" 

**Brief intro (5 seconds):**
"I’m Ronda, Nicole’s digital partner at Fern & Feather Wellness. Let’s fix that in 30 seconds."

**Main tip (15 seconds):
- "Take a 2‑minute grounding breath: Inhale for 4 seconds, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4."
- "While breathing, focus on one sensory detail – what you hear, see, or feel."
- "This simple reset drops cortisol and brings you back to the present."

**Call‑to‑action (5 seconds):
"Try it now, comment ‘Done’ if you feel the shift, and follow for more quick wellness hacks!"
