# After the Diagnosis: Adult Neurodivergent Toolkit

*Purpose:* Provide adults who have recently received a neurodivergent diagnosis (e.g., ADHD, ASD, dyslexia) with practical, science‑backed resources to navigate daily life, self‑advocacy, and emotional regulation.

---

## 1. Understanding Your Diagnosis

- **Brief Overview** – Summarize core characteristics of common neurodivergent conditions (ADHD, Autism Spectrum, Dyslexia) with APA‑cited research.
- **Neurobiology** – Explain brain differences (e.g., executive function networks, sensory processing pathways) using accessible language.
- **Common Myths** – Debunk misconceptions (e.g., “ADHD is just laziness”).

---

## 2. Self‑Assessment Worksheets

| Worksheet | Focus | How to Use |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Strengths Inventory | Identify personal strengths and talents | Complete the list, highlight top 3, discuss with therapist |
| Sensory Profile | Map sensory triggers and preferences | Rate intensity 1‑5, create a sensory‑friendly environment plan |
| Executive Function Check‑list | Pinpoint planning, organization, time‑management challenges | Prioritize 2‑3 areas for targeted strategies |

---

## 3. Practical Strategies

### A. Organization & Planning
- **Digital Tools** – Recommend apps (Notion, Todoist, Google Calendar) with brief pros/cons.
- **Paper Systems** – Use color‑coded planners, sticky‑note zones, and weekly review templates.

### B. Sensory Regulation
- **Environment Modifications** – Noise‑cancelling headphones, desk lamps with warm light, fidget tools.
- **Micro‑Breaks** – 2‑minute grounding exercises (5‑7‑8 breathing, body scan) every hour.

### C. Communication & Self‑Advocacy
- **Disclosure Script** – Sample language for informing employers/educators.
- **Rights Overview** – Brief on ADA accommodations (reasonable adjustments, workplace adjustments).

---

## 4. Emotional Support

- **Mind‑Body Techniques** – Breath work, progressive muscle relaxation, guided imagery.
- **Therapeutic Resources** – List of therapist directories, support groups (e.g., ADDA, Autism Society), and online communities.
- **Crisis Plan** – One‑page sheet with emergency contacts, coping strategies, and safe spaces.

## 6. Implementation Checklist (for self‑use)

1. ✅ Complete Strengths Inventory worksheet.
2. ✅ Set up a digital calendar with recurring reminders for micro‑breaks.
3. ✅ Choose at least two sensory regulation tools for daily use.
4. ✅ Draft a short disclosure script and practice with a trusted friend.
5. ✅ Add three supportive resources (group, therapist, online forum) to your contacts.
