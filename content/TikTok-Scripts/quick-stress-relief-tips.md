# TikTok Script: 5 Quick Stress Relief Tips

**Hook (0-3s):**
"Feeling overwhelmed? Here are 5 QUICK stress‑relief hacks you can do in under a minute!"

**Tip 1 – 4‑7s:**
*Box Breathing* – Inhale for 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Repeat twice.

**Tip 2 – 8‑11s:**
*Shake It Out* – Stand, shake arms and legs vigorously for 5 seconds. Release tension.

**Tip 3 – 12‑15s:**
*Grounding 5‑4‑3‑2‑1* – Name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

**Tip 4 – 16‑19s:**
*Mini‑Movement* – Do 5 shoulder rolls forward, then backward. Loosen the neck.

**Tip 5 – 20‑23s:**
*Cold Splash* – Splash cold water on your face or hold an ice cube for 10 seconds.

**Close (24‑30s):**
"Try ONE of these right now and feel the shift! Follow for more therapist‑approved self‑care tips."

*Caption suggestion:* "5 QUICK stress‑relief hacks you can do anywhere. #selfcare #stressrelief #therapisttips"
