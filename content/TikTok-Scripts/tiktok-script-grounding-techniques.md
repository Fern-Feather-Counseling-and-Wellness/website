# TikTok Script: Grounding Techniques for Stress Relief

**Hook (0-5s)**
- Camera on you, calm background.
- "Feeling overwhelmed? Let’s ground you in 30 seconds."

**Step 1: 5-10s – 5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Scan**
- "Look around and name 5 things you see, 4 things you hear, 3 things you feel, 2 things you smell, 1 thing you taste."

**Step 2: 10-20s – Breath Box**
- "Inhale for 4 counts, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4. Repeat twice."

**Step 3: 20-25s – Gentle Stretch**
- "Raise your arms overhead, stretch tall, then release."

**Close (25-30s)**
- "You’re back. Follow for more quick stress hacks!"

**Caption**: "Ground yourself in a minute. #StressRelief #Grounding #TherapistTips"
