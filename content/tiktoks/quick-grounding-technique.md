# TikTok Script: Quick Grounding Technique for Clients (30‑second hook)

**Hook (0‑5s):**
- "Feel overwhelmed? Let’s bring you back to the present in 30 seconds."

**Body (5‑20s):**
- "Try the 5‑4‑3‑2‑1 grounding: 
  1. Look around and name **5** things you can see.
  2. Notice **4** things you can touch and briefly touch them.
  3. Listen for **3** sounds you hear.
  4. Identify **2** smells you detect.
  5. Pinpoint **1** taste you can sense (or a sip of water)."
- "Science shows grounding reduces amygdala activation and boosts vagal tone (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021)."

**Close (20‑30s):**
- "Do this next time you’re anxious, feel the shift, and tag us @FernFeatherWellness with #GroundingReset. Let’s stay present together!"

**On‑screen text:**
- 0‑5s: "Overwhelmed? Reset now!"
- 5‑20s: "5‑4‑3‑2‑1 grounding steps"
- 20‑30s: "Tag #GroundingReset"

**Audio cue:** Calm, upbeat background music, clear voice‑over.
