- "Science says: Slow breathing and gentle movement boost vagal tone, raising HRV and easing burnout (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021)."

**Close (20‑30s):**
- "Do this before your next client and feel the shift. Tag us @FernFeatherWellness with #SomaticReset and let’s keep our nervous systems in check!"
