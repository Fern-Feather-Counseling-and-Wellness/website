# TikTok Script: Micro‑Movement for Stress Relief (30‑second hook)

**Hook (0‑5s):**
- "Therapists, feeling that tight knot in your shoulders after a long day? Let’s melt it away in 30 seconds."

**Body (5‑20s):**
- **Step 1:** Sit tall, feet flat on the floor. Inhale deeply for 4 seconds.
- **Step 2:** On the exhale, roll both shoulders up to your ears, then back and down in a smooth circle (10 seconds).
- **Step 3:** Immediately follow with a gentle neck stretch – tilt your head right, hold 2 seconds, switch left.
- **Step 4:** Finish with a 4‑7‑8 breath – inhale 4 seconds, hold 7 seconds, exhale 8 seconds.
- **Science note:** Slow, rhythmic movement activates the vagus nerve, raising heart‑rate variability and reducing cortisol (Shaffer & Ginsberg, 2021; Brown & Gerbarg, 2020).

**Close (20‑30s):**
- "Do this mini‑reset before your next client and feel the tension fade. Tag @FernFeatherWellness with #MicroMovement and let’s keep our nervous systems chill!"

**On‑screen text:**
- 0‑5s: "Tight shoulders? Reset now!"
- 5‑20s: "Shoulder roll → neck stretch → 4‑7‑8 breath"
- 20‑30s: "Tag #MicroMovement"

**Audio cue:** Calm, upbeat background music, clear voice‑over.
