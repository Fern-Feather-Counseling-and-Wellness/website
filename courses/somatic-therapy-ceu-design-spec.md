# Somatic Therapy CEU Course – Rich Infographic Slide Design Specification

**Purpose:** Provide a detailed visual guide for each of the 22 slides so a designer (or you) can build a design‑heavy, infographic‑rich PowerPoint/Google Slides deck that matches the “sage‑green, warm, subtle Fern & Feather” aesthetic you love.

**Overall Theme:**
- **Primary Palette:** Sage Green (RGB 116,130,112) – background, headings
- **Secondary Palette:** Warm Cream (RGB 250,248,240) – slide body background
- **Accent Colors:** Dark Sage (RGB 70,90,65) for headings, Accent Gold (RGB 180,150,80) for call‑outs
- **Logo:** Small Fern & Feather logo (transparent PNG) placed top‑right on every slide (≈0.8" height)
- **Typography:**
  - Heading: Montserrat Bold, 36‑48 pt, White or Dark Sage
  - Body: Montserrat Regular, 20‑24 pt, Dark Text (RGB 45,45,45)
  - Citations: Italic, 12 pt, Warm Taupe (RGB 180,170,150)
- **Layout Grid:** 12‑column fluid grid; left 2/3 for content, right 1/3 for graphics

---

## Slide‑by‑Slide Infographic Blueprint

### Slide 1 – Title Slide
- **Background:** Full‑bleed gradient: Sage Green → Warm Cream (top to bottom)
- **Center Title:** Large white text, line‑height 1.2
- **Subtitle:** Smaller cream text under title (biologist & clinician tagline)
- **Badge:** Rounded rectangle at bottom‑center: "12 CEUs | Self‑Paced Online" – Dark Sage fill, white text
- **Graphic:** Subtle abstract leaf‑vein pattern (vector) at 20 % opacity behind title

### Slide 2 – Learning Objectives
- **Header Bar:** Sage Green bar (full width, 0.8" height) with white heading "Learning Objectives"
- **Body:** Two‑column layout
  - **Left:** 6 bullet points, each with a small **icon** (check‑mark in gold) preceding text
  - **Right:** Infographic – vertical timeline showing flow from “Neurobiology” → “Polyvagal” → “Somatic Mechanisms” → “Integration”. Use simple icons (brain, vagus, heart, puzzle pieces)
- **Citation Footer:** Small italic text with NBCC approval note (bottom‑right)

### Slide 3 – Why the Body Matters in Therapy
- **Top:** Header bar same as slide 2, text “Why the Body Matters”
- **Left Column:** 5 bullets, each with a **small illustration** (e.g., silhouette with tension lines, brain‑body overlay)
- **Right Column:** Composite graphic:
  - **Center:** Human figure with overlay of nervous‑system pathways (vagus, spinal cord)
  - **Bottom‑right:** Statistic bubble: "52 % therapist burnout (Maslach, 2020)" in gold circle
- **Citation Footer:** APA style citations in taupe

### Slide 4 – Key Neuroanatomy for Somatic Therapists
- **Header:** Same style
- **Graphic‑Heavy:** Central **brain cross‑section** (vector) with numbered labels (1‑6). Around the brain, **call‑out boxes** (rounded rectangles) each containing a brief definition (e.g., "1. Brainstem – basic life functions")
- **Color Coding:** Each structure gets a subtle shade of sage (lighter for deeper structures)
- **Citation Footer** bottom‑left

### Slide 5 – Autonomic Nervous System (ANS)
- **Header:** Same
- **Graphic:** Split‑screen diagram:
  - **Left:** Sympathetic branch (red arrow, fire icon) with brief bullet list
  - **Right:** Parasympathetic branch (green leaf icon) with bullet list
  - **Center:** Small **vagus nerve** illustration linking both sides
- **Bottom Footer:** Citation

### Slide 6 – Module 2 Divider
- **Full‑bleed background:** Sage Green with large white text "Module 2: Bottom‑Up vs. Top‑Down Processing"
- **Sub‑title:** Light cream text underneath
- **Graphic:** Abstract waveform pattern representing signal flow

### Slide 7 – Hierarchical Models of Neural Processing
- **Header:** Same as prior
- **Two‑column layout:**
  - **Left:** Bullet list of top‑down & bottom‑up points (icons: upward arrow, downward arrow)
  - **Right:** **Flowchart infographic** showing arrows from PFC → Amygdala → Brainstem (top‑down) and reverse for bottom‑up. Use gradient arrows (dark sage → gold)
- **Citation Footer**

### Slide 8 – Interoception
- **Header** same
- **Graphic:** **Vagus‑to‑Insula pathway** illustration: stylized organ icons (heart, gut) with lines converging into a brain silhouette; each line labeled (e.g., "Heartbeat → NTS → Insula")
- **Body Text:** 4 bullets, each with tiny **dot** icons
- **Citation** bottom

### Slide 9 – How Bottom‑Up Drives Emotion & Behavior
- **Header** same
- **Infographic:** **Somatic marker diagram** – central human silhouette with glowing spots (gut, chest) linked by arrows to brain regions (amygdala). Use subtle glow effect (gold) for markers.
- **Bullets** on left, each describing a marker
- **Citation**

### Slide 10 – Module 3 Divider
- Full‑bleed Sage background, large white text "Module 3: Polyvagal Theory — Critical Evaluation"
- Sub‑title and abstract wave graphic (similar to slide 6)

### Slide 11 – Polyvagal Core Concepts
- **Header** same
- **Graphic:** **Three‑tier Vagal diagram** – stacked circles (ventral, sympathetic, dorsal) with icons (people chat, lightning bolt, pause symbol). Each tier includes a short caption underneath.
- **Bullet list** on right summarizing concepts
- **Citation**

### Slide 12 – Recent Critiques (2024‑2026)
- **Header** same
- **Timeline Infographic:** Horizontal timeline from 2022 → 2026 with **key study icons** (journal article, microscope) and short notes (e.g., "Grossman 2026: limited evidence"). Use gold markers for critique points.
- **Side Column:** 4 bullet points summarizing critique
- **Citation**

### Slide 13 – Module 4 Divider
- Full‑bleed Sage, text "Module 4: Evidence‑Based Somatic Techniques"

### Slide 14 – Evidence‑Based Interventions
- **Header** same
- **Graphic Grid (2 × 2):** Each cell contains:
  - Icon (e.g., wave for SE, eye for EMDR, brain for Sensorimotor, yoga pose for Yoga)
  - Title
  - Brief evidence rating (star icons, 4/5)
- **Left Column:** Intro paragraph (2 sentences) explaining criteria for evidence
- **Citation**

### Slide 15 – Mechanism Deep Dive
- **Header** same
- **Infographic:** **HRV line graph** (pre‑ vs. post‑intervention) – use gold line for improvement, teal for baseline. Include call‑out arrows pointing to spikes.
- **Bullets** on left describe each mechanism (HRV, bilateral stimulation, breathwork, movement)
- **Citation**

### Slide 16 – Module 5 Divider
- Full‑bleed Sage, text "Module 5: Why Some Somatic Techniques Fail"

### Slide 17 – Failure Modes
- **Header** same
- **Decision Tree infographic:** Starting node “Is client ready?” branching to “Window of Tolerance OK?” → “Select technique” → “Evidence‑based?” → Outcomes (green check / red cross). Use gold icons for decision points.
- **Bulleted list** on left summarizing causes of failure
- **Citation**

### Slide 18 – Module 6 Divider
- Full‑bleed Sage, text "Module 6: Integration with Evidence‑Based Practice"

### Slide 19 – Phase‑Based Treatment Model
- **Header** same
- **Flowchart:** Three phases (Stabilization → Processing → Integration) arranged horizontally with arrows. Each phase box contains **icon** (anchor for stabilization, brain for processing, puzzle for integration) and **brief bullet** (e.g., "Grounding, breathwork"). Use gradient fill (sage → cream) for each phase.
- **Citation**

### Slide 20 – Case Example: SE + CPT Integration
- **Header** same
- **Timeline graphic:** Horizontal timeline with three milestones (SE titration, CPT processing, Outcome) – each milestone illustrated with an icon (hand‑shake, thought bubble, rising arrow).
- **Bulleted summary** below the timeline
- **Citation**

### Slide 21 – References (APA 7th)
- **Header** same (but smaller font)
- **Body:** Two‑column list of references (14 items). Use **bullet‑free** formatting; each reference in italic gold text for visual contrast against warm cream background.
- **Graphic:** Small minimalistic Fern & Feather logo at bottom‑right.

### Slide 22 – Thank You / CTA
- **Full‑bleed background:** Sage Green with subtle leaf‑vein texture
- **Center Title:** "Thank You" in large white font
- **Subtitle:** "Ready to transform your practice?" – cream text
- **CTA Button:** Rounded rectangle (Accent Gold fill, Dark Sage text) – "Enroll now at fernandfeather.com/courses"
- **Logo** top‑right, small
