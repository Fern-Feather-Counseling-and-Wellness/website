## TYPOGRAPHY

- **Headings:** Bold sans-serif (Montserrat Bold or similar), 32-48pt
- **Body Text:** Regular sans-serif (Montserrat Regular or Open Sans), 18-24pt
- **Sub-headings:** Semi-bold, 20-24pt, often in goldenrod
- **Captions/Citations:** Italic, 12pt, grey

## SLIDE-BY-SLIDE DESIGN Blueprint

### SLIDE 1: Title Slide (Module 1 Divider Style)
- **Background:** Deep Forest Green (#27472C) with subtle fern photography
- **Center:** "Somatic Therapy: What's Actually Happening in the Body" in large white Montserrat Bold
- **Subtitle:** "A Science-Based Course for Mental Health Professionals" in cream
- **Bottom icons (4):** Science (brain icon), Compassion (heart), Regulation (balance), Healing (leaf)
- **Footer:** Green band with "Fern & Feather - Learning" logo

### SLIDE 2: Course Roadmap (Reference: Slide 4)
- **Top banner:** "Course Roadmap" in forest green
- **Intro paragraph:** Brief description in body text
- **Central band:** "Your Learning Journey" in peachy-tan (#E8DBCB) with woman's silhouette/brain light graphic
- **6 Module boxes:** Horizontal with icons:
  - Module 1: Brain icon — "Neuro Foundations"
  - Module 2: Body silhouette — "Bottom-Up vs Top-Down"
  - Module 3: Vagus nerve — "Polyvagal Theory"
  - Module 4: Checkmark — "Evidence-Based Techniques"
  - Module 5: Warning triangle — "Why Techniques Fail"
  - Module 6: Puzzle — "Integration"
- **Bottom sections:** "What You'll Build" and "Where This Leads"
- **Quote highlight:** "The body keeps the score — but also keeps the healing."

### SLIDE 3: Learning Objectives (Card Layout - Reference Slide 1)
- **Top banner:** "Learning Objectives" (forest green)
- **Left column (60%):** 6 objective cards with icons:
  - Icon: Checkmark (gold) — "Explain neurobiological basis..."
  - Icon: Magnifying glass — "Critically evaluate Polyvagal..."
  - Icon: Brain — "Identify physiological mechanisms..."
  - Icon: Scale — "Distinguish evidence-based..."
  - Icon: Flowchart — "Apply decision framework..."
  - Icon: Puzzle — "Integrate somatic approaches..."
- **Right column (40%):** Timeline infographic showing progression from "Foundations → Application → Integration"
- **Citation footer:** "NBCC Approved | 12 CEUs"

### SLIDE 4: Why the Body Matters (Reference Slide 2)
- **Top banner:** "Why the Body Matters in Therapy"
- **Left column:** 4 key points in card format:
  - Card 1: "Embodied Cognition" with brain-body icon
  - Card 2: "Trauma Stored Somatically" with body silhouette
  - Card 3: "Implicit Memory Access" with lock icon
  - Card 4: "Interoception" with heart icon
- **Right column:** Human body silhouette with glowing points (gut, chest, head) showing where trauma stores
- **Quote box:** "The body keeps the score." — Bessel van der Kolk (styled like reference slides)
- **Citation footer:** "van der Kolk, 2023 | Damasio, 2024"

### SLIDE 5: Key Neuroanatomy (Reference Slide 1 - HPA Axis)
- **Top banner:** "Key Neuroanatomy for Somatic Therapists"
- **Main content:** 6 labeled brain diagram sections (like HPA axis):
  - Brainstem (arousal)
  - Amygdala (threat)
  - Hippocampus (memory)
  - Prefrontal Cortex (regulation)
  - Insula (interoception)
  - ACC (conflict monitoring)
- **Style:** Numbered boxes with connecting lines (flowchart style)
- **Side call-out:** "This is adaptive." box in green with checkmark
- **Citation footer:** "Price & Hooven, 2023"

### SLIDE 6: Autonomic Nervous System (Diagram Style)
- **Layout:** Split diagram (like Slide 2 body response)
- **Left side:** Sympathetic (fight-or-flight) — terracotta accent
- **Right side:** Parasympathetic — olive green accent
- **Center:** Vagus nerve illustration connecting both
- **Bottom:** 4 quick-take cards with icons

### SLIDE 7: Module 2 Divider
- **Background:** Deep forest green with fern overlay
- **Title:** "Module 2: Bottom-Up vs. Top-Down Processing"
- **Subtitle:** "How the Body Drives Cognition and Emotion"
- **4 icons at bottom:** arrows (up/down), brain, body

### SLIDE 8: Hierarchical Processing (Flowchart)
- **Top banner:** "Hierarchical Models of Neural Processing"
- **Main infographic:** Two pathways:
  - Top-down: PFC → Amygdala → Brainstem (arrow downward, green)
  - Bottom-up: Brainstem → Amygdala → Cortex (arrow upward, terracotta)
- **Dotted feedback loop** between them
- **Side cards:** Key concepts in boxes

### SLIDE 9: Interoception (Body Diagram)
- **Layout:** Similar to Slide 2's body silhouette
- **Central visual:** Human figure with pathway from organs → vagus → brain
- **Key points:** 3 cards with icons (heart, gut, lungs)
- **Call-out:** "Interoceptive accuracy varies..." in highlighted box

### SLIDE 10: Module 3 Divider
- **Background:** Forest green with fern
- **Title:** "Module 3: Polyvagal Theory — Critical Evaluation"
- **Subtitle:** "Science, Controversy, and Nuanced Application"

### SLIDE 11: Polyvagal Core Concepts (Three-Tier Diagram)
- **Top banner:** "Polyvagal Theory: Core Concepts"
- **Main visual:** Three stacked circles (ventral, sympathetic, dorsal) — like reference slide's three-tier
- **Each tier:** Icon + description:
  - Ventral: "Social Engagement" (people icon)
  - Sympathetic: "Fight-or-Flight" (lightning)
  - Dorsal: "Shutdown" (pause)
- **Side:** Key definitions in card format

### SLIDE 12: Recent Critiques (Timeline)
- **Top banner:** "Recent Critiques (2024-2026)"
- **Timeline infographic:** Horizontal timeline with key points:
  - 2024: "Early critiques emerge"
  - 2025: "Porges response"
  - 2026: "Current state"
- **Side cards:** 4 key critique points
- **Quote box:** "Use as heuristic, not complete model."

### SLIDE 13: Module 4 Divider
- **Background:** Forest green
- **Title:** "Module 4: Evidence-Based Somatic Techniques"

### SLIDE 14: Evidence-Based Interventions (Grid Layout)
- **Top banner:** "Evidence-Based Somatic Interventions"
- **2x2 Grid of cards:**
  - SE: "Somatic Experiencing" + 4/5 star rating
  - EMDR: "Eye Movement Desensitization" + 5/5 stars
  - Sensorimotor: "Pat Ogden's Approach" + 4/5 stars
  - Yoga: "Trauma-Informed Yoga" + 4/5 stars
- **Each card:** Icon, title, brief description, evidence rating

### SLIDE 15: Mechanism Deep Dive (Graph)
- **Top banner:** "The Science of How Somatic Techniques Work"
- **Main visual:** HRV line graph (pre/post) — like reference slide's data viz
  - Baseline line (grey)
  - Post-intervention line (gold)
  - Key spike annotations
- **Side:** 4 mechanism cards with icons

### SLIDE 16: Module 5 Divider
- **Background:** Forest green
- **Title:** "Module 5: Why Some Somatic Techniques Fail"

### SLIDE 17: Failure Modes (Decision Tree)
- **Layout:** Flowchart decision tree
  - Node 1: "Client ready?"
  - Branch: Yes/No
  - Node 2: "Window of Tolerance?"
  - Node 3: "Evidence-based?"
  - Outcomes: Green check / Red X
- **Style:** Matching reference slide's "This is adaptive" box styling

### SLIDE 18: Module 6 Divider
- **Background:** Forest green
- **Title:** "Module 6: Integration with Evidence-Based Practice"

### SLIDE 19: Phase-Based Treatment (Roadmap Style)
- **Layout:** Similar to Course Roadmap (Slide 4)
- **Horizontal band:** "Phase 1: Stabilization" → "Phase 2: Processing" → "Phase 3: Integration"
- **Each phase:** Icon + brief description + arrow connectors
- **Bottom:** Key principles in card format

### SLIDE 20: Case Example (Timeline)
- **Top banner:** "Case Example: SE + CPT Integration"
- **Timeline graphic:** Horizontal with 3 milestones
  - Phase 1: SE titration (handshake icon)
  - Phase 2: CPT processing (brain icon)
  - Outcome: Recovery (upward arrow)
- **Side:** Client details in styled quote box

### SLIDE 21: References (Multi-Column)
- **Layout:** Two-column reference list
- **Style:** APA format, italic, in goldenrod/grey
- **Background:** Cream with subtle texture

### SLIDE 22: Thank You / CTA
- **Background:** Forest green with fern overlay
- **Center:** "Thank You" in large white
- **Subtitle:** "Ready to transform your practice with somatic science?"
- **CTA Button:** Goldenrod rounded rectangle: "Enroll Now"
- **Bottom:** Logo + tagline in green band
