Our Place

Our office is conveniently located in Cumming, Georgia, just one mile from GA-400 (Exit 13), making it an easy drive from Cumming, Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Suwanee, and the surrounding North Atlanta communities. We wanted therapy to happen somewhere that feels warm, human, comfortable, and a little less like a doctor's office.

Come as you are. Get comfortable.

Therapy asks enough of you already. The room shouldn't. Our space was created to feel calm without being sterile, comfortable without being precious, and welcoming without trying too hard. Settle into the couch, grab a blanket, bring your coffee, kick off your shoes if that's your thing. This is a place for real conversations with real humans.

Easy to get to. Easy to settle into.

You'll find us at 566 Peachtree Pkwy, Suite 122, Cumming, GA 30041, about one mile from GA-400 at Exit 13.

Fern & Feather Counseling and Wellness

Therapy for anxiety, OCD, trauma, and overwhelmed minds.

Fern & Feather provides warm, affirming therapy for teens and adults navigating anxiety, OCD, intrusive thoughts, overthinking, and trauma. We offer Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) and other individualized approaches in Cumming, GA and through telehealth across Georgia.
