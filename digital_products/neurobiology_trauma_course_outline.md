# Neurobiology of Trauma – CEU Course Outline

**Target Audience:** Licensed mental health clinicians (LMFT, LCSW, LPC, Psychologist) seeking CEU credit.
**Length:** 4 hours (2.5 CEUs)
**Delivery:** Self‑paced video modules + downloadable worksheets.

## Learning Objectives
1. Explain how acute and chronic trauma remodel neural circuits (amygdala, hippocampus, prefrontal cortex).
2. Describe the role of the HPA axis, cortisol, and autonomic nervous system in trauma responses.
3. Critically evaluate the evidence for polyvagal theory and recent critiques (e.g., Grossman 2026).
4. Apply neurobiological principles to design somatic interventions that regulate the nervous system.
5. Integrate biological insights into treatment planning and client education.

## Module Breakdown
### Module 1 – Foundations of Trauma Neuroscience (45 min)
- Brain structures involved in threat detection and memory consolidation.
- Neurotransmitter systems (glutamate, GABA, norepinephrine) and their dysregulation.
- Key readings: Porges 2011; Van der Kolk 2014; Grossman 2026.

### Module 2 – Stress Physiology & Allostatic Load (45 min)
- HPA axis mechanics, cortisol curves, and chronic stress impact.
- Allostatic load model and biomarkers.
- Case vignette analyses.

### Module 3 – Autonomic Regulation & Polyvagal Theory (45 min)\n- Sympathetic vs. parasympathetic pathways.
- Ventral vagal complex vs. dorsal vagal complex.
- Critical appraisal of recent literature.

### Module 4 – Somatic Interventions Grounded in Biology (45 min)
- Breathwork, grounding, and movement mapped to autonomic shifts.
- Evidence‑based protocols (e.g., Trauma‑Release Exercises, sensorimotor psychotherapy).
- Implementation worksheet.

### Module 5 – Translating Neurobiology into Clinical Practice (45 min)
- Assessment tools (neuro‑psych questionnaires, physiological monitoring).
- Designing treatment plans that incorporate biology.
- Ethical considerations & client communication.

## Assessment & CEU Credit
- 20‑question multiple‑choice quiz (passing 80%).
- Reflective exercise: write a brief case integration plan.
- Certificate of Completion generated automatically.
