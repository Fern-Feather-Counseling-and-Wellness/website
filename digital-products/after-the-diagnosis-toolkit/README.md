# After the Diagnosis – Adult Neurodivergent Toolkit

**Purpose:** A comprehensive, CEU‑eligible toolkit for adult neurodivergent clients navigating post‑diagnosis challenges. Includes psychoeducation, self‑advocacy resources, coping strategies, and a step‑by‑step implementation plan.

## Planned Modules (8)
1. Understanding Your Diagnosis – Neurobiology & Identity
2. Navigating Healthcare & Accommodations
3. Workplace Disclosure & Rights
4. Relationship Dynamics & Communication
5. Self‑Regulation & Sensory Strategies
6. Managing Co‑Occurring Mental Health
7. Building Support Networks
8. Future Planning & Goal Setting
