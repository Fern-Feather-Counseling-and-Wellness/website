# Module 6: Therapist Self‑Care & Vicarious Trauma

## 6.1 Unique Vicarious Trauma Risks
- **Emotional Contagion:** Therapists absorbing clients' sensory overwhelm, anxiety, burnout
- **Masking Overload:** Frequent exposure to clients masking can reinforce therapist's own masking
- **Chronic Sensitization:** Repeated exposure to sensory‑rich stories can lower therapist's own sensory thresholds

## 6.2 Recognizing Burnout in Therapists
- **Signs:** Emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, reduced sense of accomplishment, physical symptoms (headaches, fatigue)
- **Assessment Tools:** Professional Quality of Life (ProQOL) scale; Maslach Burnout Inventory
- **Early Warning Signs:** Increased irritability, difficulty sleeping, heightened sensory sensitivity

## 6.3 Self‑Regulation Strategies for Clinicians
- **Box Breathing & Tactical Breathing:** Quick regulation during sessions
- **Sensory Breaks:** Scheduled micro‑breaks between clients; use of quiet rooms
- **Boundary Setting:** Limiting caseload of high‑sensory‑need clients; scheduling "low‑stimulus" days
- **Peer Supervision:** Regular consultation with neurodivergent peers for insight and support

## 6.4 Building Cultural Competence & Humility
- **Ongoing Education:** Workshops on neurodiversity, sensory processing, intersectionality
- **Reflective Practice:** Examining personal biases, assumptions about "normal" behavior
- **Supervision & Consultation:** Seek supervision focused on neurodivergent affirming practice

## 6.5 Exercise: Personal Resilience Plan
- **Activity:** Complete the "Therapist Resilience Plan" worksheet (provided in the worksheets folder).
- **Components:** Identify triggers, create sensory‑healthy routines, schedule supervision, set caseload limits.
