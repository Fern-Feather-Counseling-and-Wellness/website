# Module 4: Misdiagnosis – Anxiety vs. Autism vs. ADHD vs. Trauma

## 4.1 Symptom Overlap: A Systematic Review
- **Common Shared Features:** Social anxiety, sensory sensitivities, attention difficulties, emotional dysregulation
- **Key Distinctions:**
  - *Anxiety:* Fear of judgment; seeks reassurance; improves with exposure
  - *Autism:* Social communication differences; rigid thinking; sensory overload triggers
  - *ADHD:* Inattention, impulsivity; reward‑sensitivity; forgetfulness vs. executive dysfunction
  - *Trauma:* Hypervigilance, flashback, avoidance tied to specific memories; numbing

## 4.2 Assessment Instruments & Their Limits
- **Screening Tools:** AQ‑10, RAADS‑R (autism); ASRS‑5 (ADHD); GAD‑7, PHQ‑9 (anxiety); PCL‑5 (trauma)
- **Limitations:** Self‑report may be confounded by masking, alexithymia, or trauma responses
- **Gold‑Standard Assessment:** Multidisciplinary evaluation; developmental history, observation, collateral records

## 4.3 Differential Diagnosis Frameworks
- **Developmental Timeline:** Early‑onset vs. later‑onset; trajectory of symptom emergence
- **Contextual Factors:** School, work, relationships – patterns of strength and challenge
- **Co‑occurring Conditions:** High comorbidity; need to identify all that apply (e.g., ADHD + anxiety + autism)

## 4.4 Cultural & Gender Considerations
- **Gender Bias:** Women often misdiagnosed due to masking; late diagnosis
- **Cultural Variance:** Cultural norms affect presentation; assessment tools may lack validation for diverse groups
- **Intersectionality:** Race, ethnicity, socioeconomic status impact diagnostic pathways

## 4.5 Exercise: Differential Diagnosis Checklist
- **Activity:** Complete the "Neurodivergent Differential Diagnosis" worksheet; compare presentation across categories.
- **Goal:** Practice ruling in/out conditions based on symptom profile and history.
