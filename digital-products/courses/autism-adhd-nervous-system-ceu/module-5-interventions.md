# Module 5: Interventions & Accommodations

## 5.1 Sensory‑Informed Therapeutic Techniques
- **Sensory Diets in Therapy:** Co‑creating sensory schedules; using fidgets, weighted lap pads, lighting adjustments
- **Environmental Modifications:** Low‑stimulus waiting areas, noise‑reducing headphones, visual schedules
- **Mindfulness & Regulation:** Adapted breathing exercises, body scan, yoga nidra for nervous system regulation

## 5.2 Therapeutic Modalities Adapted for Neurodivergence
- **CBT Adjustments:** Concrete, visual cues; focus on anxiety reduction rather than cognitive restructuring
- **DBT for Autism/ADHD:** Emphasis on emotion regulation, distress tolerance, impulse control; shorter modules
- **DIR/Floortime:** Developmental, individual‑differences, relationship‑based model for children and adults
- **ACT (Acceptance & Commitment Therapy):** Values‑based action; flexible approach suited for varied sensory needs

## 5.3 Pharmacological Considerations
- **Medication Overview:** Stimulants (ADHD), non‑stimulants, SSRIs (anxiety), antipsychotics (autism aggression)
- **Role of Therapist:** Monitoring side effects, supporting medication adherence, non‑prescriptive guidance
- **Recent Research (2023‑2026):** Pharmacogenomics guiding medication selection; new ASD medication (e.g., balovaptan)

## 5.4 Accommodations & Advocacy
- **IEP / 504 Plans:** School‑based accommodations
- **Workplace Accommodations:** ADA protections; request process; job‑coach supports
- **Self‑Advocacy Training:** Teaching clients to articulate needs, request accommodations

## 5.5 Exercise: Intervention Planning Template
- **Activity:** Design a 4‑session treatment plan for a client with autism & anxiety, using sensory‑informed CBT.
- **Include:** Session goals, sensory accommodations, homework assignments, outcome measures.
