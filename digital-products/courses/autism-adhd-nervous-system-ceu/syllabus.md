---

## Course Overview

This course addresses the urgent need for therapists to understand autism and ADHD beyond diagnostic labels, focusing on the underlying nervous system physiology. Participants will explore sensory processing differences, autistic burnout, misdiagnosis, and evidence‑based interventions tailored to neurodivergent clients. The curriculum emphasizes practical strategies for clinicians who feel underprepared to support this growing population.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon completion, participants will be able to:

1. Define autism and ADHD through a neurobiological lens, differentiating between behavioral presentation and underlying nervous system processes.
2. Explain sensory processing & overload physiology, including hyper‑ and hypo‑sensitivity, interoceptive differences, and their impact on daily functioning.
3. Recognize autistic burnout and its relationship to allostatic load, energy accounting, and nervous system exhaustion.
4. Distinguish between anxiety, autism, ADHD, and trauma through a neurodivergent‑informed differential diagnosis framework.
5. Apply sensory‑informed accommodations and therapeutic interventions to support neurodivergent clients.
6. Identify vicarious trauma risks and develop self‑care strategies when working with neurodivergent populations.
