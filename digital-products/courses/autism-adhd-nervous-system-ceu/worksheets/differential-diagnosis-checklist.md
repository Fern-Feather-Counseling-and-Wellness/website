### Decision Rules
- If **Autism** columns have strong marks across social communication, sensory, and restricted behaviors → consider autism.
- If **ADHD** columns dominate inattention and impulsivity → consider ADHD.
- If **Anxiety** columns are strongest in worry, physiological arousal, and avoidance tied to fear → consider anxiety disorder.
- If **Trauma** columns show flashbacks, intrusion, and clear traumatic events → consider PTSD/complex trauma.
