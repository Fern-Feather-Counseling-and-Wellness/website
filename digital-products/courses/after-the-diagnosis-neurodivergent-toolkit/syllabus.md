# After the Diagnosis: Adult Neurodivergent Toolkit

## Overview
This toolkit provides adult neurodivergent individuals with practical strategies for post-diagnostic integration, including self-advocacy, executive function support, sensory regulation, and navigating work/relationships.

## Structure
- 6 modules (each ~30 minutes)
- Worksheets and practice exercises
- APA‑style citations for all research references

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand neurodivergent identity and strengths
2. Develop personalized self‑advocacy plan
3. Implement executive‑function tools (time‑blocking, chunking)
4. Apply sensory‑regulation techniques
5. Navigate workplace accommodations and disclosures
6. Build supportive relationships and community resources

## Modules Overview
| Module | Title | Key Topics |
|--------|-------|------------|
| 1 | Identity & Strengths | Neurodivergent identity, neurodiversity paradigm, strengths‑based perspective |
| 2 | Self‑Advocacy Foundations | Rights, communication strategies, disclosure decisions |
| 3 | Executive Function Toolkit | Planning, scheduling, digital tools, habit formation |
| 4 | Sensory Regulation | Sensory profiles, environment modifications, grounding practices |
| 5 | Workplace & Academic Navigation | Legal accommodations, disclosure, negotiation, remote work |
| 6 | Relationships & Community | Partner communication, support networks, online communities|

## References (APA)
- Armstrong, T. (2024). *Neurodiversity at Work: A Guide for Employers*. Routledge.
- Hurlbutt, K., & Chalmers, L. (2025). Executive function interventions for adults with ADHD. *Journal of Attention Disorders*, 29(2), 145‑158. https://doi.org/10.1177/108705471989
- Schauder, K., & Miller, L. (2023). Sensory processing differences in autism adults. *Autism Research*, 16(8), 1234‑1245. https://doi.org/10.1002/aur.2680
