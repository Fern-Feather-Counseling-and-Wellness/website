# Module 2: The Brain's Fear Circuitry

## 2.1 The Amygdala: The Brain's Alarm Center
- **Central Nucleus:** Coordinates autonomic and behavioral responses (fight-or-flight).
- **Basolateral Complex:** Involved in fear conditioning and memory formation.
- **Amygdala Hijack:** How the amygdala overrides cortical processing in acute anxiety.
- **Hyperactivity in Anxiety:** Neuroimaging studies showing enlarged or overactive amygdala in GAD, PTSD, and panic.
- **Lateral Amygdala:** Processes sensory inputs and detects threats rapidly.

## 2.2 The Hippocampus: Context and Memory
- **Spatial Memory:** How the hippocampus aids in contextualizing threat.
- **Contextual Fear Conditioning:** Learning to associate neutral contexts with threat.
- **Stress-Induced Atrophy:** Chronic stress reduces hippocampal volume; implications for memory in anxiety.
- **Pattern Separation:** How the hippocampus distinguishes safe vs. threatening contexts.

## 2.3 The Prefrontal Cortex (PFC): The Regulator
- ** ventromedial PFC (vmPFC):** Inhibits amygdala activity; impaired in anxiety.
- ** dorsolateral PFC (dlPFC):** Cognitive control, working memory; downregulated in chronic anxiety.
- **Top-Down Regulation:** How the PFC "talks down" the amygdala.
- **Impaired Top-Down Control:** The disconnect between PFC and amygdala in anxiety disorders.

## 2.4 The Anterior Cingulate Cortex (ACC)
- **Error Detection:** The ACC monitors conflicts and potential threats.
- **Hyperactivity in GAD:** ACC shows heightened activity during worry.
- **Cognitive Control:** The ACC is crucial for shifting attention away from threat.

## 2.5 Key Neurotransmitter Systems
- **GABA (Gamma-Aminobutyric Acid):**
    - Primary inhibitory neurotransmitter.
    - Benzodiazepines enhance GABA activity.
    - GABA deficits linked to heightened anxiety states.
- **Serotonin (5-HT):**
    - Modulates mood, arousal, and impulse control.
    - SSRIs increase synaptic serotonin, reducing anxiety.
    - Complex role: Not simply "low serotonin."
- **Norepinephrine:**
    - Main neurotransmitter of the sympathetic nervous system.
    - Hyperactivity contributes to panic symptoms (tachycardia, sweating).
    - Locus coeruleus is the main source.
- **Glutamate:**
    - Primary excitatory neurotransmitter.
    - Dysregulation can lead to hyperexcitability and anxiety.
    - NMDA and AMPA receptors play a role.

## 2.6 The Fear Circuit: Integrated Model
- **Thalamus → Amygdala → Hypothalamus:** Fast, subcortical pathway (粗糙, quick).
- **Thalamus → Sensory Cortex → Amygdala:** Slower, cortical pathway (detailed processing).
- **PFC → Amygdala:** Top-down inhibition.
- **Clinical Implication:** Anxiety disorders often involve a hyperactive "low road" and/or a hypoactive "high road."
