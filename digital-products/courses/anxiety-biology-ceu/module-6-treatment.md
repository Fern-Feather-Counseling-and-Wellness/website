# Module 6: Biological Mechanisms of Treatment

## 6.1 Pharmacological Interventions
- **SSRIs (Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors):**
    - Increase synaptic serotonin.
    - Take weeks to work (BDNF increase, neurogenesis).
    - First-line treatment for most anxiety disorders.
    - Examples: Fluoxetine, Sertraline, Escitalopram.
- **SNRIs (Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitors):**
    - Increase serotonin AND norepinephrine.
    - Venlafaxine, Duloxetine.
- **Benzodiazepines:**
    - Enhance GABA-A receptor activity.
    - Fast-acting, but risks of tolerance, dependence, withdrawal.
    - Not first-line for chronic anxiety.
- **Buspirone:**
    - Partial dopamine agonist.
    - Anxiolytic with delayed onset.
    - No dependence/tolerance.
- **Beta-Blockers:**
    - Block peripheral symptoms of anxiety (propranolol).
    - Useful for performance anxiety.
- **Mirtazapine:**
    - NaSSA (Noradrenergic and Specific Serotonergic Antidepressant).
    - Anxiolytic, can help with sleep and appetite.

## 6.2 Psychological Interventions: Neurobiological Effects
- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT):**
    - Top-down regulation: Strengthens PFC control over amygdala.
    - Neuroimaging shows increased PFC activity post-CBT.
    - Reduces amygdala reactivity to threat.
- **Exposure Therapy:**
    - Habituation (neural fatigue of fear response).
    - Extinction learning (new safety associations).
    - Memory reconsolidation.
- **Mindfulness-Based Therapies:**
    - Increases PFC thickness and activity.
    - Reduces amygdala reactivity.
    - Enhances vagal tone (HRV).
- **EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing):**
    - Bilateral stimulation may enhance integration.
    - Affects prefrontal cortex and amygdala.

## 6.3 Somatic Interventions: Biological Mechanisms
- **Breathwork:**
    - Slow breathing activates parasympathetic nervous system.
    - Extended exhale stimulates vagus nerve.
- **HRV Biofeedback:**
    - Direct training of parasympathetic tone.
    - Increases baroreflex sensitivity.
- **Exercise:**
    - Increases BDNF (neurogenesis).
    - Reduces cortisol.
    - Increases serotonin and endorphins.
- **Sleep:**
    - Critical for memory consolidation (including extinction learning).
    - Sleep deprivation increases anxiety.

## 6.4 Combination Approaches
- **Medication + Therapy:** Often more effective than either alone.
- **Biological Rationale:** Medication can "take the edge off" enough to allow therapy to work.
- **Sequential Treatment:** Medication for stabilization, then therapy for skill-building.

## 6.5 The Clinician's Role
- **Informed Prescribing:** Understanding the biology helps in medication selection.
- **Psychoeducation:** Explaining the "why" improves adherence.
- **Holistic Treatment Planning:** Integrating lifestyle, medication, and therapy.
