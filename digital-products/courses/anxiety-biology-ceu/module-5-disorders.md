# Module 5: Biological Basis of Key Anxiety Disorders

## 5.1 Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)
- **Core Features:** Persistent, diffuse worry about multiple domains.
- **Neurobiological Correlates:**
    - ACC hyperactivity during worry.
    - Reduced prefrontal inhibition of amygdala.
    - Altered GABA signaling.
- **Genetics:** High heritability, polygenic.
- **Treatment:** SSRIs, SNRIs, buspirone, CBT.

## 5.2 Panic Disorder
- **Core Features:** Recurrent, unexpected panic attacks.
- **The "Locus Coeruleus":** Brainstem region critical for panic.
- ** lactate Sensitivity:** Elevated lactate can trigger panic in susceptible individuals.
- **Carbon Dioxide (CO2) Hypersensitivity:** Inhaled CO2 triggers panic attacks.
- **Somatic Symptoms:** Derealization, depersonalization (出自miture).
- **Treatment:** SSRIs, benzodiazepines, CBT (including interoceptive exposure).

## 5.3 Social Anxiety Disorder (Social Phobia)
- **Core Features:** Fear of social scrutiny.
- **Amygdala Hyperactivity:** In response to social stimuli (faces).
- **Self-Focus:** heightened self-referential processing in the medial PFC.
- **Serotonin Dysregulation:** Linked to social avoidance.
- **Treatment:** SSRIs, CBT, exposure therapy.

## 5.4 Specific Phobias
- **Core Features:** Marked fear of specific object/situation.
- **Classical Conditioning:** Learning (tone + shock = fear).
- **Preparedness:** Evolutionary predisposition to fear certain stimuli (snakes, spiders).
- **Amygdala Activation:** Specific to phobic stimulus.
- **Treatment:** Exposure therapy (habituation), VR exposure.

## 5.5 PTSD & Complicated Grief
- **Hyperarousal:** Dysregulated sympathetic nervous system.
- **HPA Axis:** Blunted cortisol response.
- **Amygdala Hyperactivity:** Heightened threat detection.
- **Hippocampal Atrophy:** Impaired context memory.
- **Treatment:** SSRIs (sertraline, paroxetine), Prazosin (nightmares), EMDR, CBT.

## 5.6 OCD (Anxiety-Related)
- **Core Features:** Intrusive thoughts, compulsions.
- **Cortico-Striatal-Thalamic Circuit:** Dysfunction in OCD.
- **Serotonin & Dopamine:** Both implicated.
- **Treatment:** SSRIs, clomipramine, CBT (ERP), Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS).
