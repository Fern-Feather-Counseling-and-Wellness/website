# Module 3: The HPA Axis & Stress Response

## 3.1 Overview of the HPA Axis
- **Hypothalamus:** Releases Corticotropin-Releasing Hormone (CRH).
- **Pituitary:** Secretes Adrenocorticotropic Hormone (ACTH).
- **Adrenal Glands:** Release cortisol (cortisol's role in stress response).
- **Negative Feedback Loop:** How cortisol shuts down the stress response.

## 3.2 Cortisol: The Stress Hormone
- **Diurnal Rhythm:** Cortisol is highest in the morning, lowest at night.
- **Allostatic Load:** The cumulative "wear and tear" from chronic stress.
- **Cortisol and Anxiety:** Both elevated and blunted cortisol responses are seen in different anxiety disorders.
- **Glucocorticoid Receptors:** How cortisol regulates its own release.

## 3.3 The Autonomic Nervous System (ANS)
- **Sympathetic Nervous System (SNS):** "Fight-or-flight" – increases heart rate, dilates pupils, inhibits digestion.
- **Parasympathetic Nervous System (PNS):** "Rest-and-digest" – conserves energy, promotes relaxation.
- **Vagal Tone:** The resting tone of the vagus nerve; linked to emotional regulation.
- **Low Vagal Tone:** Associated with anxiety, depression, and poor emotion regulation.

## 3.4 Allostasis and Allostatic Load
- **Allostasis:** The process of achieving stability through physiological or behavioral change.
- **Allostatic Load:** The cumulative cost of allostatic overload.
- **Consequences of High Allostatic Load:**
    - Metabolic syndrome
    - Cardiovascular disease
    - Immune suppression
    - Cognitive decline
- **Measuring Allostatic Load:** Biomarkers including cortisol, IL-6, CRP, blood pressure.

## 3.5 HPA Axis Dysregulation in Anxiety Disorders
- **HPA Axis in PTSD:** Hyperresponsive CRH, blunted cortisol response.
- **HPA Axis in GAD:** Altered negative feedback sensitivity.
- **HPA Axis in Panic Disorder:** Dysregulated cortisol awakening response.
- **Early Life Stress:** Programming the HPA axis for hyperreactivity.

## 3.6 Clinical Applications: Regulating the Stress Response
- **Lifestyle Interventions:** Sleep, exercise, diet.
- **Somatic Interventions:** Breathwork, HRV biofeedback, yoga.
- **Pharmacological:** SSRIs, SNRIs, pregabalin.
- **Psychoeducation:** Teaching clients about their stress response.
