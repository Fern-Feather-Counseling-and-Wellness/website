# Private Practice Launchpad for Therapists

## Module 3: Finances & Pricing

**Learning Objectives:**
*   Set profitable and sustainable rates for your services.
*   Understand the financial logistics of private practice, including insurance vs. private pay.
*   Create a basic financial plan and budget for your practice.

**Content:**

### 3.1 Setting Your Rates
*   **Market Research:** Analyzing rates in your area and niche.
*   **Value-Based Pricing:** Charging what you're worth based on your expertise and overhead.
*   **Sliding Scale Considerations:** Ethical and practical approaches to accessibility.
*   **Rate Adjustment Strategies:** When and how to raise your rates.
*   **Exercise:** *Rate Setting Worksheet* - Calculating your minimum viable income and desired profit.

### 3.2 Insurance vs. Private Pay
*   **Insurance Paneling:** Pros, cons, and the application process (Caes, United, etc.).
*   **Out-of-Network (OON) & Superbills:** How to work with clients who have OON benefits.
*   **Private Pay Model:** Building a practice without insurance dependence.
*   **Hybrid Approaches:** Combining insurance and private pay.

### 3.3 Financial Systems for Private Practice
*   **Tracking Income & Expenses:** Simple spreadsheets or accounting software (QuickBooks, Wave).
*   **Separate Business Accounts:** Setting up a business checking account.
*   **Tax Obligations:** Estimated taxes, self-employment tax, and deductions.
*   **Budgeting for Stability:** Planning for slow seasons and unexpected expenses.

**Worksheet:**
*   *Financial Planning & Pricing Strategy Worksheet*

## Module 5: Ethical Practice & Operations

**Learning Objectives:**
*   Implement essential policies for privacy, informed consent, and record-keeping.
*   Choose and set up a practice management system (EHR).
*   Prepare for common ethical dilemmas in private practice.

**Content:**

### 5.1 Informed Consent & Policies
*   **The Informed Consent Document:** Essential components (confidentiality, limits, fees, cancellation).
*   **Telehealth Consent:** Specific considerations for online sessions.
*   **Social Media & Technology Policies:** Defining boundaries for client contact.
*   **Financial & Cancellation Policies:** Setting expectations upfront.

### 5.2 Privacy & Record-Keeping
*   **HIPAA Compliance:** Protecting client health information.
*   **Electronic Health Records (EHR):** Choosing a secure platform (SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, Jane).
*   **Data Security:** Passwords, encryption, and secure wifi practices.
*   **Retention & Disposal:** How long to keep notes and how to destroy them.

### 5.3 Managing Boundaries & Self-Care
*   **Work-Life Balance:** Setting hours, taking vacations, and avoiding burnout.
*   **Supervision & Consultation:** Maintaining ethical competence.
*   **Continuing Education:** Staying current with licensure requirements.

## Module 6: Building Your Practice (The First 90 Days)

**Learning Objectives:**
*   Execute a launch plan for your first clients.
*   Manage the logistics of a new practice efficiently.
*   Evaluate progress and adjust your strategy.

**Content:**

### 6.1 The Soft Launch
*   **Announcing Your Practice:** Reaching out to your network.
*   **Low-Cost Marketing Tactics:** Leveraging your existing community.
*   **Intake Process:** Creating a seamless experience for new clients.
*   **First Session Structure:** Setting the tone for the therapeutic relationship.

### 6.2 Managing Your Schedule
*   **Scheduling Best Practices:** Buffer time, note-taking, and self-care between sessions.
*   **No-Shows & Cancellations:** Handling missed appointments professionally.
*   **Financial Tracking:** Invoicing, collecting payments, and following up.

### 6.3 Scaling Your Practice
*   **Evaluating Success:** Metrics to track (inquiries, conversion rates, client retention).
*   **Feedback Loops:** Asking clients for feedback (anonymous surveys).
*   **Growth Strategies:** Adding groups, workshops, or supervisees.
*   **Planning for Year One:** Setting realistic goals and celebrating wins.
