# Parenting Neurodivergent Kids: A Nervous System Approach

## Module 7: Collaboration with Schools

**Learning Objectives:**
*   Understand effective communication strategies with school personnel.
*   Prepare for and participate effectively in IEP/504 meetings.
*   Know how to advocate for appropriate accommodations and services.

**Content:**

### 7.1 Building Bridges with Educators
- **Establishing a Positive Relationship:** Beginning with requests for evaluation and open communication.
- **Effective Communication:** Using clear, objective language; focusing on collaboration, not confrontation.
- **School Meetings:** IEPs, 504 Plans, and informal check-ins.
- **Key Players:** Understanding the roles of teachers, administrators, psychologists, special education coordinators.

### 7.2 Navigating the IEP/504 Process
- **Your Rights as a Parent:** IDEA, Section 504, state-specific regulations.
- **Evaluation Request:** Crafting a strong request letter.
- **The IEP Meeting:** Preparation, participation, and decision-making.
- **The 504 Plan:** Accommodations for students not eligible for special education.

### 7.3 Advocating for Your Child
- **Data Collection:** Using tracking logs to support your requests.
- **Accommodation vs. Modification:** Understanding the difference and necessity.
- **Behavior Support Plans (PBSP):** Creating effective strategies for challenging behaviors.
- **Resolving Disputes:** Informal strategies and formal options (mediation, state complaint).

**Exercise:**
- Draft an IEP meeting preparation checklist.
- Role-play challenging conversation scenarios with school staff.

## Module 8: Building Family Resilience

**Learning Objectives:**
*   Develop strategies for managing parental stress and preventing burnout.
*   Foster sibling understanding and support.
*   Create a family system that embraces neurodivergence.

**Content:**

### 8.1 Parental Self-Care & Regulation
- **The Parent's Nervous System:** Recognizing and managing caregiver stress.
- **Sustainable Self-Care:** Moving beyond quick fixes to integrated practices.
- **Building a Support Network:** Connecting with other parents and professionals.

### 8.2 Siblings of Neurodivergent Children
- **Understanding Sibling Dynamics:** Addressing potential challenges (resentment, guilt, overwhelm).
- **Educating Siblings:** Age-appropriate explanations of neurodivergence.
- **Promoting Empathy & Connection:** Strategies for fostering positive sibling relationships.

### 8.3 Creating a Neurodiversity-Affirming Home
- **Shifting the Narrative:** From "fixing" to "supporting" and "understanding."
- **Environmental Adjustments:** Sensory-friendly spaces and routines.
- **Celebrating Neurodivergent Strengths:** Acknowledging and nurturing unique talents.
- **Family Identity:** Embracing neurodivergence as a valuable part of the family's identity.

**Exercise:**
- Develop a family self-care plan.
- Create a "Family Neurodiversity Affirmation" statement.

## Course Conclusion

You've embarked on a powerful journey of understanding and advocacy. Remember that collaboration, patience, and a focus on your child's unique strengths are key. You are their most important advocate, and with the right tools and support, you can help them thrive.
