## Module 3: Evidence-Based Interventions I

### 3.1 Overview of Evidence-Based Trauma Treatments
- **Hierarchy of Evidence:** RCTs, meta-analyses, and practice guidelines
- **APA Division 12 Guidelines:** Strong research support for PTSD treatments
- **Matching Treatment to Client:** Factors influencing treatment selection

### 3.2 Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)
- **The Model:** Challenging trauma-related beliefs (Resick et al., 2017)
- **Protocol Overview:** 12-session structure
- **Stuck Points:** Identifying and challenging cognitive distortions
- **Trauma Narrative:** Writing and processing the traumatic experience
- **Research Support:** Strong efficacy for PTSD (CASLAT included)
- **Adaptations:** CPT-A for adolescents, CPT for military populations

### 3.3 Prolonged Exposure (PE)
- **Foa's Theory:** Emotional processing of fear structures (Foa et al., 2007)
- **Techniques:**
  - *In Vivo Exposure:* Gradual confrontation with safe but avoided stimuli
  - *Imaginal Exposure:* Revisiting the trauma memory through storytelling
  - *Processing:* Making sense of the experience
- **Research Support:** Robust evidence for PTSD reduction
- **Contraindications:** Active substance use, severe dissociation, suicidality

### 3.4 Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR)
- **Shapiro's Theory:** Adaptive Information Processing (AIP) model (Shapiro, 2018)
- **The 8-Phase Protocol:**
  1. History taking & treatment planning
  2. Preparation (resource installation)
  3. Assessment (target identification)
  4. Desensitization (bilateral stimulation)
  5. Installation (strengthening adaptive memory)
  6. Body scan (residual targeting)
  7. Closure (returning to balance)
  8. Reevaluation
- **Research Support:** Strong efficacy, now a first-line treatment
- **Training Requirements:** Basic training (25 hours) required for ethical use

### 3.5 Comparison of First-Line Treatments
| Feature | CPT | PE | EMDR |
|---------|-----|----|------|
| Focus | Cognitive | Emotional | Information processing |
| Trauma Narrative | Required | Required | Optional |
| Specific Training | Minimal | Moderate | Required |
| Client Readiness | Moderate | High | Moderate |

**References:**
- Foa, E. B., Hembree, E. A., & Rothbaum, B. O. (2007). *Prolonged exposure therapy for PTSD: Emotional processing of traumatic experiences*. Oxford University Press.
- Resick, P. A., Monson, C. M., & Chard, K. M. (2017). *Cognitive processing therapy for PTSD: A comprehensive manual*. Guilford Press.
- Shapiro, F. (2018). *Eye movement desensitization and reprocessing (EMDR) therapy: Basic principles, protocols, and procedures* (3rd ed.). Guilford Press.

## Module 4: Evidence-Based Interventions II

### 4.1 Mindfulness-Based Interventions
- **Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR):** Kabat-Zinn's foundational program
- **Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT):** Preventing depression relapse
- **Trauma-Sensitive Mindfulness:** Treleaven's guidelines for safe practice
- **Research:** Meta-analyses showing reduction in PTSD symptoms (Goldberg et al., 2018)

### 4.2 Somatic Experiencing (SE)
- **Levine's Approach:** Pendulation and discharge of trapped survival energy
- **Key Concepts:**
  - *Tracking:* Noticing body sensations
  - *Pendulation:* Oscillating between resource and activation
  - *Discharge:* Facilitating completion of defensive responses
- **Evidence:** Preliminary support, growing research base
- **Training:** 3-year professional training program

### 4.3 Internal Family Systems (IFS)
- **Schwartz's Model:** Parts work and Self leadership
- **Core Concepts:**
  - *Exiles:* Wounded parts carrying trauma
  - *Managers:* Protective parts managing daily life
  - *Firefighters:* Blunting emotional pain
  - *Self:* The core of calm, curiosity, and compassion
- **Protocol:** Finding, focusing, floating, and harnessing
- **Research:** Growing evidence for PTSD and complex trauma (Anderson et al., 2021)

### 4.4 Narrative Exposure Therapy (NET)
- **For Refugees & Complex Trauma:** Creating a coherent narrative of one's life
- **Protocol:** Life mapping, detailed testimony, burial of the dead
- **Evidence:** Strong support for refugee populations (Robjant & Fazel, 2010)

### 4.5 Skills-Based Approaches
- **DBT for Trauma:** Emotion regulation and distress tolerance
- **ACT for Trauma:** Acceptance, defusion, valued action
- **Skills Training in Affective and Interpersonal Regulation (STAIR):** For childhood trauma

**Exercise:** *Intervention Selection Role-Play* - Practice introducing different modalities to clients

**References:**
- Anderson, R., Bice, D., & Phelps, L. (2021). The effectiveness of Internal Family Systems therapy in the treatment of trauma: A meta-analysis. *Journal of Family Psychotherapy*, 32(3), 267-289.
- Goldberg, J., et al. (2018). Mindfulness-based stress reduction for PTSD: A meta-analysis. *Journal of Clinical Psychology*, 74(12), 2009-2022.
- Robjant, K., & Fazel, M. (2010). The emerging evidence for narrative exposure therapy: A review of meta-analytic results. *BMC Psychiatry*, 10, 48.

## Module 6: Vicarious Trauma & Self-Care

### 6.1 Understanding Vicarious Trauma
- **Definition:** Also known as compassion fatigue
- **The Parallel Process:** How therapist exposure affects clinical work
- **Symptoms:**
  - Intrusive images or thoughts
  - Hyperarousal or numbing
  - Altered worldview (hypervigilance, hopelessness)
  - Changes in identity or spirituality

### 6.2 Assessing Your Risk
- **Self-Assessment Tools:** Professional Quality of Life (ProQOL) Scale
- **Warning Signs:** Personal indicators of vicarious trauma
- **Contributing Factors:** Caseload, trauma type, supervision quality, personal history

### 6.3 Building Resilience
- **Organizational Supports:** Supervision, consultation, caseload management
- **Individual Practices:**
  - *Box Breathing:* Regulation in moments of activation
  - *Boundaries:* Saying no, taking breaks
  - *Pleasure Activities:* Recharging outside of work
  - *Peer Support:* Connecting with colleagues
- **Ongoing Monitoring:** Regular check-ins with yourself

### 6.4 Creating a Sustainable Practice
- **Caseload Management:** Setting limits, taking call, vacation policies
- **Supervision & Consultation:** Non-negotiable elements
- **Continuing Education:** Investing in growth, not just requirement
- **Career Longevity:** Planning for a long, healthy career

### 6.5 The Business Case for Self-Care
- **Ethical Imperative:** Impairment and client welfare
- **Financial Considerations:** Burnout costs, career longevity
- **Quality of Life:** Beyond the therapy room
