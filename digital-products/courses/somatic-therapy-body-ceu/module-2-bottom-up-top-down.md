# Module 2: Bottom‑Up vs. Top‑Down Processing

## 2.1 Hierarchical Models of Neural Processing
- **Top‑Down Processing:** Higher cortical regions (PFC, ACC) influence lower subcortical regions (amygdala, brainstem) through descending inhibition.
- **Bottom‑Up Processing:** Subcortical structures send signals upward, influencing emotional states, perceptions, and behaviors before higher cortical appraisal.
- **Clinical Implication:** Trauma often disrupts top‑down regulation, leaving clients stuck in bottom‑up reactivity (e.g., flashbacks, emotional flooding).

## 2.2 Interoception: The Body Talking to the Brain
- **Definition:** The sense of the internal physiological state of the body (visceral sensations, hormone levels).
- **Neural Pathways:** Signals travel from internal organs via the vagus nerve to the nucleus tractus solitarius (NTS) → thalamus → insula → somatosensory cortex.
- **Interoceptive Accuracy:** Variabilities in how accurately individuals perceive internal states; links to anxiety, alexithymia, and trauma.

## 2.3 How Bottom‑Up Processing Drives Emotion & Behavior
- **Somatic Markers:** Gut feelings, "butterflies," chest tightness as embodied predictions.
- **Threat Detection:** Rapid subcortical pathways (thalamus → amygdala) allow fast, pre‑conscious threat detection before cortical appraisal.
- **Implication for Therapy:** Clients may need to resolve body‑based (bottom‑up) arousal before cognitive (top‑down) processing is possible.

## 2.4 Top‑Down Regulation Strategies
- **Cognitive Reappraisal:** Using the PFC to re‑interpret emotional stimuli.
- **Mindfulness‑Based Approaches:** Strengthening PFC‑amygdala connectivity through meta‑cognitive awareness.
- **Evidence:** Meta‑analyses show cognitive strategies reduce amygdala reactivity (2023‑2025).

## 2.5 Integrating Bottom‑Up and Top‑Down Approaches
- **Phase‑Based Treatment:** Stabilize (bottom‑up) → Process (top‑down) → Integrate.
- **Sequential Use:** Begin with somatic regulation (breathwork, grounding) to create window of tolerance; then introduce cognitive processing.
- **Case Example:** Using SE for titration (bottom‑up) before CPT cognitive work (top‑down).
