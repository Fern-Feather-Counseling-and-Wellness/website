## Worksheet: Intervention Selection Flowchart

**Purpose:** To match client presentation to evidence‑based somatic interventions.

**Instructions:** Follow the flowchart to select the most appropriate technique.

---

### Step 1: Assess Readiness for Somatic Work
- **Is the client able to stay within their window of tolerate?**
  - **Yes** → Go to Step 2.
  - **No** → Use stabilization first (grounding, breathwork) → Re‑assess.

### Step 2: Identify Primary Problem Domain
- **Trauma memories / PTSD symptoms?** → **EMDR** (strongest evidence).
- **Chronic arousal / autonomic dysregulation?** → **Breathwork / HRV biofeedback**.
- **Somatic symptoms / body-held tension?** → **Somatic Experiencing** or **Sensorimotor Psychotherapy**.
- **Overall regulation / need for adjunctive movement?** → **Trauma‑informed yoga**.

### Step 3: Consider Comorbidities
- **Comorbid anxiety + trauma?** → **EMDR** + adjunctive yoga.
- **Comorbid depression + trauma?** → **EMDR** + behavioral activation.
- **Severe dissociation?** → **Stabilization** (SE) before EMDR.

### Step 4: Check Contraindications
- **Active substance use?** → Stabilize first.
- **Severe dissociation?** → Avoid intense exposure; use SE stabilization.
- **Medical contraindication to breathwork?** → Use gentle movement instead.

### Step 5: Determine Sequence
- **Start:** Bottom‑up (stabilize).  
- **Middle:** Process (EMDR, SE).  
- **End:** Integration (cognitive processing, meaning‑making).
