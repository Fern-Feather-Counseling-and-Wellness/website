# Module 6: Integrating Somatic Work with Evidence‑Based Practice

## 6.1 Case Conceptualization: When to Add Somatics
- **Decision Framework:** Use bottom‑up when client is stuck in top‑down only; use top‑down when somatic work may cause flooding.
- **Indicators:** Chronic arousal despite CBT; alexithymia; body‑focused somatic complaints; difficulty accessing emotions verbally.

## 6.2 Sequencing Bottom‑Up and Top‑Down Interventions
- **Phase Model:**
  1. **Stabilization:** Grounding, breathwork, pendulation (bottom‑up).
  2. **Processing:** Cognitive processing, exposure, narrative (top‑down).
  3. **Integration:** Combined approaches; meaning‑making; relapse prevention.

## 6.3 Hybrid Approaches in Action
- **SE + CPT:** Use SE for titration before cognitive processing of trauma beliefs.
- **EMDR + Somatic:** Add body scan during EMDR reprocessing to monitor somatic shifts.
- **Yoga + CBT:** Use yoga for arousal reduction before cognitive restructuring of anxiety beliefs.

## 6.4 Measuring Outcomes
- **Standardized Measures:** PCL‑5 (PTSD), GAD‑7 (anxiety), PHQ‑9 (depression), PHQ‑15 (somatic symptoms).
- **Physiological Markers:** HRV, skin conductance, cortisol.
- **Session‑Level Feedback:** SESSION ( subjective units of disturbance ) ratings; body‑awareness logs.

## 6.5 Ethical & Professional Considerations
- **Training Requirements:** Only practice techniques within scope and competence; refer for specialized somatic training.
- **Documentation:** Record somatic interventions, client responses, rationale.
- **Informed Consent:** Be transparent about evidence base; discuss potential risks/benefits.

## 6.6 Future Directions
- **Research Gaps:** Need for large RCTs comparing sequencing of bottom‑up vs. top‑down; optimal integration models.
- **Technology:** Wearable HRV biofeedback, VR‑augmented somatic interventions (emerging 2025‑2026).

## Exercise: Integrated Treatment Plan
- **Activity:** Design a 6‑session treatment plan for a client with PTSD and chronic back pain, incorporating evidence‑based somatic (e.g., yoga, EMDR) and CBT components.
- **Include:** Session‑by‑session goals, interventions, outcome measures.
