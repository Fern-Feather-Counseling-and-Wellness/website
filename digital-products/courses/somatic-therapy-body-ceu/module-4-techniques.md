# Module 4: Evidence‑Based Somatic Techniques

## 4.1 Somatic Experiencing (SE)
- **Developer:** Peter Levine
- **Core Mechanisms:** Pendulation (oscillating between resource and activation), discharge of trapped survival energy, tracking body sensations.
- **Evidence:** Preliminary support; growing research base (Levine, 2024). Mixed RCT evidence; need for larger trials.
- **Clinical Application:** Use for clients with chronic tension, frozen states, or difficulty verbalizing trauma.

## 4.2 Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR)
- **Developer:** Francine Shapiro
- **Core Mechanisms:** Adaptive Information Processing (AIP); bilateral stimulation facilitating memory reprocessing.
- **Evidence:** Strong efficacy for PTSD; now a first‑line treatment (APA guidelines). Neuroimaging shows changes in amygdala and PFC activity (2023‑2025).
- **Clinical Application:** Standard 8‑phase protocol; useful for trauma processing when client has adequate window of tolerance.

## 4.3 Sensorimotor Psychotherapy (SP)
- **Developer:** Pat Ogden
- **Core Mechanisms:** Integrating body‑based interventions with cognitive and emotional processing.
- **Evidence:** Emerging evidence for trauma; combines bottom‑up and top‑down.
- **Clinical Application:** Useful for clients who are "in their heads" and need embodied interventions.

## 4.4 Trauma‑Informed Yoga & Mind‑Body Practices
- **Evidence:** Meta‑analyses show modest to moderate effect on PTSD symptoms (Goldin & Gross, 2025).
- **Mechanisms:** Improves parasympathetic tone, interoceptive awareness, emotion regulation.
- **Clinical Application:** Adjunctive tool; not a standalone trauma treatment.

## 4.5 Breathwork & Biofeedback
- **Techniques:** Diaphragmatic breathing, coherent breathing, box breathing.
- **Evidence:** HRV biofeedback shows moderate efficacy for anxiety and depression (2024).
- **Clinical Application:** Simple, accessible tools for autonomic regulation; can be taught in session.

## 4.6 Comparative Table

| Technique | Primary Target | Evidence Level | Key Mechanism |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| EMDR | Trauma memories | Strong | Bilateral stimulation → AIP |
| SE | Survival energy discharge | Moderate | Pendulation, discharge |
| SP | Cognitive‑body integration | Emerging | Bottom‑up + top‑down |
| Yoga | Overall regulation | Moderate | Parasympathetic activation |
| Breathwork | Autonomic balance | Moderate | HRV increase |
