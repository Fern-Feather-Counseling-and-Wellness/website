## Agenda Items

**1. Welcome and Introductions (5-10 min)**
*   Brief introductions of all attendees.
*   Recap of meeting purpose.
*   Confirmation of confidentiality and respectful communication ground rules.

**2. Parent/Guardian Concerns and Perspective (15-20 min)**
*   [Your Name(s)] to present concerns, observations, and desired outcomes.
*   Reference relevant documentation (e.g., Parent Input Statement, tracking logs).

**3. School Team's Perspective and Data Review (15-20 min)**
*   Review of child's current progress, assessment data, and existing interventions.
*   Presentation of current IEP/504 plan or proposed changes.
*   School staff to share observations and insights.

**4. Focused Discussion on Key Issues (30-45 min)**
*   **Item 1:** [e.g., Discuss specific academic challenges and proposed supports]
    *   Proposed solutions/strategies:
    *   Discussion and brainstorming:
*   **Item 2:** [e.g., Address behavioral concerns and effective interventions]
    *   Proposed solutions/strategies:
    *   Discussion and brainstorming:
*   **Item 3:** [e.g., Review accommodation effectiveness and necessary adjustments]
    *   Proposed solutions/strategies:
    *   Discussion and brainstorming:

**5. Agreement on Next Steps and Action Items (15-20 min)**
*   Summarize agreements reached.
*   Assign specific action items to individuals (Who will do What by When?).
*   Specify follow-up communication or review dates.

**6. Closing (5 min)**
*   Confirm understanding of action items and timelines.
*   Schedule next check-in or meeting if necessary.
*   Express commitment to collaboration.

---

**Notes:**
*   This agenda is a guide; flexibility is key.
*   Focus on collaborative problem-solving.
*   Remember the goal is to support the child's educational success.
