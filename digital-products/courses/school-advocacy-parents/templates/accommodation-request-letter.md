These requests are based on [mention reasons, e.g., observed needs, teacher feedback, existing diagnosis like ADHD/Autism/Dyslexia, or request for evaluation for such].

We are requesting the following accommodations:

**Academic Accommodations:**
*   [ ] **Extended Time:** Provide additional time (e.g., 1.5x, 2x) for assignments, tests, and projects.
*   [ ] **Reduced Assignments:** Shorten the length or number of required assignments.
*   [ ] **Alternative Formats:** Provide notes, lectures, or assignments in a preferred format (e.g., graphic organizers, outlines, audio recordings, text-to-speech).
*   [ ] **Use of Technology:** Allow use of assistive technology such as spell checkers, grammar checkers, calculators, or text-to-speech/speech-to-text software.
*   [ ] **Breaks:** Frequent short breaks during instruction or testing to reset and refocus.
*   [ ] **Preferential Seating:** Seat [Child's Name] in a location with minimal distractions (e.g., front of the room, away from windows/doors).
*   [ ] **Modified Instruction:** Break down complex tasks, provide clear, concise instructions, use visual aids.
*   [ ] **Organizational Support:** Assistance with organizing materials, assignment tracking, or planner use.
*   [ ] **[Add other specific academic accommodations needed]**

**Environmental/Behavioral Accommodations:**
*   [ ] **Movement Breaks:** Allow planned opportunities for movement throughout the day.
*   [ ] **Sensory Support:** Access to a quiet space, fidget tools, noise-reducing headphones, or permission to wear comfortable clothing.
*   [ ] **Positive Behavior Support:** Focus on reinforcing desired behaviors and providing support during challenging moments.
*   [ ] **Visual Schedules:** Use of visual schedules to support transitions and predictability.
*   [ ] **Clear Directions:** Provide explicit instructions for tasks and expectations.
*   [ ] **[Add other specific environmental/behavioral accommodations needed]**
