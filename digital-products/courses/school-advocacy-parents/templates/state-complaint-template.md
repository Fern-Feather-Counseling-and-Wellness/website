**Nature of the Violation(s):**
[Clearly and concisely describe the specific violation(s) of IDEA or state law. Be factual and objective. Refer to specific incidents, dates, and individuals involved. If possible, reference specific sections of IDEA or state regulations that were violated. Be as detailed as possible.]

**Examples of potential violations:**
*   **Failure to evaluate:** School district failed to conduct an evaluation within the legally mandated timeline after receiving a written request on [Date].
*   **Denial of FAPE:** The provided educational program or services are not reasonably calculated to provide educational benefit.
*   **Procedural Violations:** Failure to provide prior written notice, failure to conduct an IEP/504 meeting within timelines, failure to include parent(s) as required team members, failure to provide all legally required documents in advance of meetings.
*   **Incorrect Placement/LRE:** Child is not educated in the least restrictive environment appropriate to their needs.
*   **Non-implementation of IEP/504 Plan:** Services, accommodations, or modifications outlined in the plan are not being provided as written.
*   **Discriminatory practices:** Exclusionary discipline, failure to accommodate disability-related behaviors.
