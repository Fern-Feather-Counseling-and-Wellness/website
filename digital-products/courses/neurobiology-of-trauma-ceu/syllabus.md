# Neurobiology of Trauma: CEU Course Syllabus

**Course Title:** Neurobiology of Trauma: A Clinician's Guide
**Credits:** 12 CEU (NBCC approved, APA pending)
**Format:** Self‑paced online (video, readings, worksheets)
**Target Audience:** LPC, LCSW, LMFT, Psychologists, Counselors

## Course Outline

| Module | Title | Estimated Time |
|--------|------------------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | Foundations of Trauma & the Brain | 1 hour |
| 2 | The Stress Response System – Biochemistry | 1 hour |
| 3 | Neuroplasticity – Rewiring the Traumatized Brain | 1 hour |
| 4 | Memory Systems & Traumatic Recall | 1 hour |
| 5 | Neurobiological Correlates of Trauma‑Related Disorders | 1 hour |
| 6 | Neurobiology of Evidence‑Based Interventions | 1 hour |
| 7 | Assessment, Final Assignment, & Certificate | 1 hour |

**Learning Objectives** – see `neurobiology-of-trauma-ceu.md` for full list.

**Materials** – PDFs for each module, worksheets (available in the `worksheets` subfolder), video links (to be recorded).

**Assessment** – 30 multiple‑choice questions + a 500‑word reflective assignment.

**Certificate** – Issued on successful completion.
