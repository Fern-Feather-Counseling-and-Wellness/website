# TikTok Script: Trauma‑Informed Yoga (30‑Second Hook)

**Opening Hook (0‑5 s):**
- *[Camera on you, smiling]* "Did you know your yoga practice can actually help rewire trauma in your brain? Let me show you how in 30 seconds!"

**Quick Science Bite (5‑12 s):**
- *[Cut to simple graphic of brain with nervous system]* "When you move mindfully, you stimulate the vagus nerve, shifting your body from a stress‑response (sympathetic) to a calm‑response (parasympathetic)."

**Key Pose Demonstration (12‑25 s):**
- *[Demonstrate a gentle pose, e.g., Child’s Pose]* "Try this: Kneel, sit back on your heels, stretch your arms forward, and breathe deep. Stay here for three breaths, feeling the stretch in your lower back. It signals safety to your nervous system."

**Call‑to‑Action (25‑30 s):**
- *[Return to face cam]* "Give this a try tomorrow after a tough session and notice the calm. Follow for more trauma‑informed yoga tips! #YogaTherapy #TraumaRecovery"

---

**Notes for Production:**
- Keep camera steady; use a soft background.
- Add subtitles for accessibility.
- Include a gentle, calming soundtrack (no more than 10 dB).
