Not sure what your plan covers?
That's completely normal — mental health benefits can be confusing. During your free consultation, we'll verify your coverage and walk you through your exact cost before your first session, so there are no surprises.

We also keep a few sliding-scale spots open for clients who need them, with priority for marginalized communities. If cost is a concern, let's talk — we'll do our best to find an option that works for you.

📄 Under the No Surprises Act, you have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate explaining how much your care will cost. We're happy to provide this upon request.
