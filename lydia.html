Hi, I’m Lydia

I’m so excited to be joining the Fern & Feather team and to have the opportunity to play a part in helping connect people with the support and resources they need. I have my Bachelor’s degree in Education, and while my primary role these days is being home with my young kids, I’m excited to bring my love of learning, connecting with others, and helping people into this new chapter of theirs.

Life Outside the Office

Outside of work, my favorite role is being a wife and mom. I absolutely love spending time with my husband and our kids, and we’re always up for a little adventure! Some of my favorite places to visit are Walt Disney World and Universal Studios! There’s just something about a theme park day that never gets old!

When I’m not chasing after my kids or planning our next adventure, you can usually find me outside enjoying nature, baking something sweet, or dreaming about our next trip. I’m so happy to be part of the Fern & Feather team and help make a difference in people’s lives!

My Role at Fern & Feather 🌿

As Fern & Feather’s Office Administrator, I’m here to help make the experience of connecting with our practice feel as welcoming and straightforward as possible. There can be a lot of little details involved in getting started with therapy, and I’m happy to be one of the friendly faces behind the scenes helping everything come together.

You may hear from me when you’re first getting connected with Fern & Feather, have questions about the practice, need help with administrative details, or aren’t quite sure who to reach out to. I work alongside our clinicians to help keep things organized and make sure you know where to go when you need something.

Most importantly, I want people to feel comfortable reaching out. Whether you have a quick question or need a little help figuring out your next step, you don’t have to worry about knowing exactly what to ask or having everything figured out before you contact us. I’m happy to help point you in the right direction.
