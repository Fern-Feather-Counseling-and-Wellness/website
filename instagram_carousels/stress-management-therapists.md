# Instagram Carousel: Stress Management for Therapists

**Slide 1**
*Title:* Why Therapists Need Self‑Care
- You give so much to clients, but your nervous system still feels the strain.
- Burnout, compassion fatigue, and secondary trauma are real.
- Investing in *you* protects your clients.

**Slide 2**
*Title:* The Therapist Stress Cascade
- Client shares trauma → your mirror neurons fire → HPA axis spikes.
- Without recovery, cortisol stays high → sleep issues, irritability.
- *Science:* Shapiro et al., 2022: therapists using self‑regulation have lower cortisol.

**Slide 3**
*Title:* 3‑Minute Grounding Reset (Box Breathing)
- Inhale 4 s, hold 4 s, exhale 4 s, hold 4 s.
- Do it *between* sessions or after a heavy client.
- Triggers the vagus nerve, shifts you to parasympathetic mode.
