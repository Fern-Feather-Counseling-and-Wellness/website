# Instagram Carousel: Neurobiology of Trauma

**Slide 1:** *"What actually happens in the brain during trauma?"*
- Quick hook: "Your brain goes into survival mode – here’s the science behind it."

**Slide 2:** *"Amygdala alarm system"*
- The amygdala detects threat and fires the fight‑or‑flight response.
- Quick fact: It can hijack rational thinking in seconds.

**Slide 3:** *"Prefrontal cortex shuts down"*
- Stress hormones (cortisol, norepinephrine) impair the prefrontal cortex.
- Result: Decision‑making, empathy, and self‑regulation dip.

**Slide 4:** *"Hippocampus memory scramble"*
- Trauma overloads the hippocampus, leading to fragmented memories.
- You might feel stuck reliving moments without context.

**Slide 5:** *"How to reset the nervous system"*
- Simple tip: 4‑4‑4‑4 box breathing for 30 seconds.
