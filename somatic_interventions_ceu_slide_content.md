# Somatic Interventions CEU Slide Deck

## Slide 1: Title
**Title:** Somatic Interventions for Talk Therapists (2 CEUs)
**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand the neuroscience behind somatic change
- Master core somatic skills for clinical use
- Apply somatic techniques across diagnoses

## Slide 2: Why Talk Therapy Needs the Body
- Talk alone often leaves clients stuck in physiological survival states
- Trauma and chronic stress are stored in the body (Van der Kolk, 2014)
- Insula and ANS dysregulation drive symptoms (Paulus & Stein, 2006)
**Image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Autonomic_nervous_system.svg

## Slide 3: Integrated Brain Networks
- Survival circuits, emotion, and executive function are integrated (Pessoa, 2017)
- Key structures: Brainstem, Amygdala, Insula, PFC
**Image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/55/Brain_network.svg/1200px-Brain_network.svg.png

## Slide 4: Autonomic Nervous System Overview
- Sympathetic: fight‑or‑flight
- Parasympathetic: rest‑and‑digest
- PNS includes the vagus (Porges, 2011)
**Image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Autonomic_nervous_system.svg

## Slide 5: Interoception & Insula
- Insula processes internal bodily signals
- Altered insula activity in PTSD and anxiety (Paulus & Stein, 2006)
**Image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/44/Insular_cortex.png

## Slide 6: Core Skill – Tracking Autonomic Arousal
- Observe breathing, heart rate, muscle tension
- Use simple scales (0‑10) to rate arousal in session

## Slide 7: Grounding Techniques
- 5‑5‑5 (5 senses, 5 seconds each)
- Feet‑on‑earth, heavy‑object hold
- Physiological rationale: re‑engage somatosensory cortex
**Image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6a/Sine_wave.gif (placeholder)

## Slide 8: Pendulation & Titration
- Move between safety and activation in controlled doses
- Maintain window of tolerance

## Slide 9: Orienting & Defensive Completion
- Orient: bring client into present moment
- Defensive completion: finish unfinished defensive actions

## Slide 10: Introducing Somatics to Clients
- Explain rationale: body‑mind integration
- Normalize the approach and set expectations

## Slide 11: Somatic Inquiry Scripts
- Ask: "What does this feel like in your body?"
- Invite client to notice sensations without judgment
- Link sensation to emotion or thought

## Slide 12: Clinical Applications – Anxiety & Panic
- Use grounding to reduce hyperarousal
- Track breath and heart rate during exposure
- Introduce titration for gradual exposure
**Image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/8/84/Autonomic_nervous_system.svg

## Slide 13: Clinical Applications – Trauma & PTSD
- Pendulation to stay within window of tolerance
- Somatic inquiry to process stored trauma sensations
- Defensive completion for unresolved defensive actions
**Image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/55/Brain_network.svg/1200px-Brain_network.svg.png

## Slide 14: Clinical Applications – Depression & Shutdown
- Gentle grounding to re‑engage body
- Interoceptive awareness to notice numbing
- Use movement to counteract inactivity

## Slide 15: Scope of Practice & Ethics
- Stay within therapist licensure limits
- When to refer to somatic specialists
- Document somatic interventions in notes

## Slide 16: Documentation & Progress Notes
- Record bodily sensations observed
- Note client’s self‑report of interoceptive experience
- Link to treatment goals and outcomes

## Slide 17: When to Refer
- Client shows signs of dissociation or unsafe trauma material
- Complex somatic issues beyond therapist training
- Need for specialized body‑based therapy

## Slide 18: Resources & Further Training

- Sensorimotor Psychotherapy (Pat Ogden)
- Trauma‑Informed Yoga certifications
