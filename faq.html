Frequently Asked Questions

Rooted and Rising

Fern & Feather Counseling and Wellness

What should I expect in my first session?

Mostly, a conversation. We'll talk about what's bringing you in, what you hope might change, and whatever feels important to share. You don't need a polished life story or a five-year healing plan. We're getting to know each other, and you're figuring out whether we're a good fit.

Can I swear in session?

Fuck, yes. You can swear, cry, laugh, ramble, or say you have absolutely no idea where to start. This isn't a job interview, and we're not grading your vocabulary.

What if I don't know what to talk about?

That's okay. You don't need to arrive with an agenda, a breakthrough, or even a coherent sentence. We can start with how your week went, what's on your mind, or the fact that you don't know what to say.

What if I cry?

We have tissues. Lots of tissues. You can cry, not cry, laugh at a wildly inappropriate moment, or feel nothing in particular. There's no correct way to have feelings in this room.

What issues do you work with?

Anxiety, OCD, trauma, depression, relationships, identity, life transitions, and the many complicated ways being human can get exhausting. Different clinicians have different specialties, so we'll help you figure out whether we're a good fit.

Can I talk about sex, identity, or relationships that don't fit the usual mold?

Absolutely. LGBTQ+ identities, kink, non-monogamy, sex, and complicated relationships are all welcome topics. We're not here to clutch pearls or make assumptions about your life.

What if I disagree with my therapist?

Please tell us. Therapy isn't a dictatorship with throw pillows. You're allowed to question an idea, say something didn't land, or ask to try a different approach. Your feedback matters.

What modalities do you use?

It depends on the clinician and what you need. Our approaches may include CBT, DBT, ERP, IFS-informed work, somatic approaches, attachment-focused work, and more. We'll talk about what we're doing and why—no mystery treatment plan required.

Am I going to have to talk about my childhood?

Maybe, if it's relevant and you're comfortable going there. But we're not going to blame every inconvenience in your adult life on your mother because you mentioned brunch.

How long does therapy take?

There's no universal timeline. Some people come for a specific concern; others want longer-term support. We'll check in about your goals and progress along the way. You're not signing up for a lifetime subscription.

What if I've tried therapy before and hated it?

Fair. A therapist or approach that wasn't right for you doesn't mean therapy can't be helpful. You're welcome to be skeptical, ask questions, and tell us what didn't work last time.

Are you going to tell me to just think positively?

Absolutely fucking not. Sometimes things genuinely suck. We're more interested in understanding what's happening and helping you navigate it than putting a motivational quote on top of it.

Collaboration with Other Providers

At Fern & Feather Counseling and Wellness, we believe in a collaborative approach to your wellbeing. We regularly coordinate with psychiatrists, primary care physicians, nutritionists, acupuncturists, and other wellness practitioners (with your written consent) to ensure you receive comprehensive, integrated care that honors all aspects of your health.

Therapy for anxiety, OCD, trauma, and overwhelmed minds.

Fern & Feather provides warm, affirming therapy for teens and adults navigating anxiety, OCD, intrusive thoughts, overthinking, and trauma. We offer Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) and other individualized approaches in Cumming, GA and through telehealth across Georgia.
