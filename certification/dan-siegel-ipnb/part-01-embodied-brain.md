## Core Concepts

### 1. Neural Tube Development
- Ectoderm (outer layer) → forms both skin AND nervous system
- Your brain is literally "sophisticated elaborated skin cells" (Siegel's phrasing)
- Neural tube → spinal cord → brain clusters

### 2. Genetics & Gene Expression
- DNA → RNA → protein = how genes actually do something
- "If the gene is never expressed, it's like a book nobody reads"
- Genes determine brain structure in utero (human brain vs. chimp vs. mouse)

### 3. Epigenetics
- Histones + methyl groups sit on top of DNA, control whether genes get read
- Swedish famine studies and Holocaust intergenerational research are real
- Timing/gender matters: effects can pass to granddaughters if the pregnant mother experienced trauma

### 4. Temperament
- Innate nervous system propensities: sensory sensitivity, novelty tolerance, mood baseline, rhythmicity
- Genetically and epigenetically influenced

### 5. Neuroplasticity
- ~100 billion neurons, each with ~10,000 synaptic connections
- Myelin speeds up signaling 3000x
- "Neurons that fire together wire together" — Hebb (1949), Carla Shatz paraphrased
- **Therapy gold:** "Where attention goes, neural firing flows and neural connection grows"
