## Core Concepts

### The **T** of PART – Trust
- **Presence** → openness
- **Attunement** → focusing on another’s internal world
- **Resonance** → feeling the shared vibration
- **Trust** → the state that emerges when the above three are present

### Threat vs. Receptive States
| State | Response | Effect on Trust |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Threat** (no‑trust) | Fight, Flight, Freeze, Collapse | Shuts down openness, connection, and self‑regulation |
| **Receptive** (trust) | Presence, attunement, resonance → safety | Enables neuroception of safety, activates the **social engagement system** (Polyvagal Theory) |

### Polyvagal & Neuroception (Siegel ↔ Porges)
- **Neuroception** = brain’s automatic appraisal of safety (pre‑frontal circuits) → activates either the **ventral vagal** (social engagement) or defensive branches.
- **Default Mode Network (DMN)** → midline pre‑frontal + posterior nodes; active at rest, monitors “others in the self” (OATS).
- Over‑active DMN → anxiety, rumination, chaotic/rigid states (Siegel’s “OATS overload”).
- **Empathic statements** → brief moments of presence/attunement can boost immune response (controlled study: cold recovery 1‑day faster) – **validated** (clinical empathy improves outcomes). 

### Rupture & Repair
- **Rupture** = any break in the four PART elements (presence, attunement, resonance, trust).
- **Repair** = pause, acknowledge, take responsibility, and re‑establish the missing element.
- **Benign rupture** – e.g., an urgent phone call; can be repaired with a quick apology.
- **Toxic rupture** – e.g., ignoring a partner for trivial reasons; can cause lasting distrust.

### Clinical Takeaways
1. **Assess** whether client is in a threat (chaos/rigidity) or receptive (trust) state.
2. **Coach** presence → attunement → resonance → trust.
3. **Watch** for over‑active DMN / OATS overload – encourage grounding, breathing to shift neuroception.
4. **Model** repair: acknowledge mis‑attunement, own responsibility, invite the client to co‑create repair.
