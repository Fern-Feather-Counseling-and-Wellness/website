## ⚖️ Integration Framework

### What Is Integration?
- **Differentiation** — parts are distinct and specialized
- **Linkage** — parts are connected and communicating
- **Integration** = differentiation + linkage → coherent, flexible flow

### What Happens Without Integration?
| Impairment | Result |
|------------|--------|
| Impaired linkage | **Chaos** — too connected, overwhelmed |
| Impaired differentiation | **Rigidity** — too separated, frozen |
| Both impaired | Dysfunction (all DSM symptoms) |

### The Healing Move
- Move toward integration: help client differentiate AND connect
- Not "fixing" — facilitating self-organization

## 🤝 The PART Framework (Relationships)

| Element | Definition | Example |
|---------|------------|---------|
| **Presence** | Full attention, open to receiving | Eye contact, not distracted |
| **Attunement** | Aligning with another's internal world | "I see you're upset about..." |
| **Resonance** | Feeling the shared vibration | Mirror emotions appropriately |
| **Trust** | Receptive state that emerges | Client feels safe to open up |

### Rupture & Repair
- **Rupture** — any break in presence/attunement/resonance/trust
- **Repair** — acknowledge, take responsibility, re-connect
- **Benign rupture** — forgivable (emergency phone call)
- **Toxic rupture** — violates trust (dismissing, mocking)

## 🏥 Clinical Applications

### For Therapists
1. **Assess** — Is the client in chaos (flooded) or rigidity (dissociated)?
2. **Educate** — "Your brain is changeable" (neuroplasticity)
3. **Facilitate integration** — Differentiate + link
4. **Model PART** — Be present, attuned, resonant, trustworthy
5. **Repair actively** — Name ruptures, invite repair
6. **Watch for burnout** — Differentiate from clients' pain (empathy vs. compassion)

### For Clients (Psychoeducation)
- "Your nervous system is wired by experience, but can change"
- "Distress is either too much (chaos) or too little (rigidity) — we want flow"
- "Connection heals — being seen, soothed, safe, secure"
