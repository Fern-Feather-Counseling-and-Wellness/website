## 🎯 Design Tips

1. **Keep it simple** — one concept per slide
2. **Use icons** — they're faster to process than text
3. **Bold numbers** — 100B neurons, 3000x, #1 predictor
4. **Question format** — engages more than statements
5. **Color code:** Blue = brain, Green = integration, Orange = relationship
