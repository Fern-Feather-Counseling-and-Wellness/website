## Core Concepts

### The Four Facets of Mind

**1. Information Processing**
- How we problem-solve, think, direct attention
- Happens with or without awareness

**2. Consciousness**
- The experience of being aware
- Can influence how we process information

**3. Subjective Experience**
- The "felt texture" of lived life
- The inner quality of experience

**4. Self-Organization** ⭐ (The New One)
- The emergent self-organizing, embodied and relational process that regulates the flow of energy and information
- This is the KEY differentiator in Siegel's model

## Complex Systems Theory

### What Makes a System "Complex"?

| Feature | Meaning | Example: You |
|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Open** | Influenced by things outside boundaries | You absorb world around you |
| **Chaos-capable** | Random, unpredictable | Your chaotic days |
| **Non-linear** | Small input → large unpredictable output | Flat tire → whole day derailed |

### The Key Insight: Emergence
- "The whole is greater than the sum of its parts"
- A cloud's shape cannot be predicted by studying individual air molecules
- Similarly, mind emerges from energy flow — cannot be reduced to brain parts

### Self-Organization (Recursive!)
- Self-organization arises from complex systems naturally
- **It regulates what it arises from** — recursive feedback loop
- This is mathematically established, not hypothesis
- Cloud shapes = self-organization (no programmer needed!)
- Mind = same principle applied to embodied relational system
