## The 5 Ws + H of Mind

### 1. WHAT — What is Mind?
- **Proposal:** Mind is an emergent property of energy and information flow
- Four facets: information processing, consciousness, subjective experience, self-organization
- Siegel frames this as possibility, not definitive proof

### 2. WHERE — Where is Mind?
- **Within** — inside your body/brain
- **Between** — in relationships with others
- **Distributed mind** — you can literally feel the emergent field in a room
- Anthropologist Peter Sge's "social field" concept
- NOT just brain activity in skull

### 3. WHEN — When Does Mind Exist?

**The Arrow of Time Problem:**
- Classical physics (large objects): directionality of change exists (can't unbreak an egg)
- Quantum physics (subatomic): NO arrow of time

**Siegel's Proposal:**
- Microstates of energy (quantum level) = timeless "eternal now"
- Macrostates (thoughts, feelings, bodies) = have arrow of time
- Mind has BOTH: timeless presence AND linear time (past/future)
- This explains why meditation feels "timeless" but also why we age/worry

**Clinical insight:** Reflective practices can give access to the timeless microstate experience

### 4. WHO — Who Are We?

**Two modes of being:**

| Mode | What It Is | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Conduit** | Letting patterns flow through you | Walking on beach, sensing without constructing |
| **Constructor** | Building narratives, memories, hopes | Remembering the past, imagining the future |

- **Balancing conduit and constructor** is key to wellbeing
- "Presence" = info processing close to the thing itself (conduit)
- Over-construction = anxiety, rumination

### 6. HOW — How Does Mind Function in Wellness vs. Unwellness?

**The Core Pattern:**
- **Chaos** — overwhelmed, dysregulated, unpredictable
- **Rigidity** — shut down, inflexible, frozen
- Prolonged chaos or rigidity = dysfunction

**Integration = Health:**
- Integration = linking of differentiated parts → FACILITATED flow
- Impaired linkage OR impaired differentiation → creates chaos or rigidity

**The Big Claim:**
> "All symptoms of all syndromes in the DSM can be redefined as examples of chaos or rigidity"

| Disorder | Chaos Example | Rigidity Example |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| PTSD | Intrusive memories, emotional flooding | Dissociation, avoidance |
| Bipolar | Manic racing thoughts | Depressive shutdown |
| Depression | Rumination | Flat affect, stuck thinking |
| Anxiety | Panic, overwhelm | Rigid worry loops |

**Research Support:**
- Neurodevelopmental disorders (autism, schizophrenia, bipolar): impaired integration in brain
- Experienced-based disorders (abuse, neglect): impaired integration
- **Human Connectome Project:** Interconnected connectome = best predictor of wellbeing ✅

## The Integration Framework

### What is Integration?
- **Differentiation** — parts are distinct
- **Linkage** — parts are connected
- **Integration** = differentiation + linkage → coherent flow

### What Impairs Integration?
- Trauma (can't link)
- Neglect (can't differentiate)
- Both create chaos or rigidity

### What Heals?
- Creating conditions for integration to emerge
- Not "fixing" — facilitating self-organization
