## Key Rationales

### Q6: Interpersonal Integration
Integration = differentiation + linkage. Dismissing another's inner state = the opposite of integration (no differentiation, no linkage).

### Q7: Mindsight
Mindsight = seeing mind in self and others, promoting integration. Shutting off communication is anti-mindsight.

### Q11: Differentiation + Linkage
This is THE core concept of integration in relationships. Without both, you get chaos (too much linkage) or rigidity (too much differentiation).

### Q12: Therapist's Empathy
Siegel emphasizes that empathy + responsiveness is what creates the safe harbor for integration to happen.

### Q13: Integration Increases Brain Connectivity
The Human Connectome Project research shows integration correlates with better connectivity.

### Q15: Prolonged Negative Emotions = Decreased Integration
Sadness, anger, fear, shame = chaos or rigidity. Both are impaired integration.

### Q19: Joy/Love = Increased Integration
Positive emotions correlate with integrated brain states.

### Q21: Impaired Integration = Chaos + Rigidity
This is directly from Siegel's framework.

### Q22: MWe
The integrated sense of "Me + We" = MWe

### Q24: Body Height
Genetics, experience, culture shape the brain — not body height.

### Q29: Rigidity
Rigidity = lack of integration. Flexibility, coherence, adaptive capacity = integrated.

### Q32: Coan & Johnson
fMRI study showing holding partner's hand reduces fear response (less prefrontal + limbic activation).

### Q38: Imago Therapy
Hendrix/Hunt — we select partners who match our unresolved childhood wounds.

### Q40: Avoidant + Preoccupied
Classic anxious-avoidant trap: preoccupied pursues, avoidant withdraws → frustration → breakup.
