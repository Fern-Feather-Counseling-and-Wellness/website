# Fern & Feather Pricing Model

## Overview

This document outlines the proposed pricing structure for Fern & Feather Wellness Center once we launch the private practice. The goal is to balance high-value, private‑pay rates with market competitiveness while maintaining the premium, science‑based brand positioning.

## Service Tiers

| Service | Duration | Rate (USD) | Rationale |
|---------|----------|------------|-----------|
| Individual Therapy | 50 min | $225 | Premium expertise, limited client load (max 12 clients) ensures deep focus and high availability. |
| Couples Therapy | 75 min | $300 | Joint sessions require additional preparation and coordination. |
| Group Therapy (4‑6 participants) | 90 min | $150 per participant | Scalable offering; lower per‑person cost while still delivering high‑impact somatic work. |
| Trauma‑Informed Yoga Workshop (2 h) | $100 per participant | Small group format, integrates yoga with trauma‑informed principles. |
| Private‑Practice Supervision (1 h) | $150 | For LPC candidates and early‑career therapists seeking mentorship. |
| Digital Product (Workbook, Course) | $49‑$299 | Tiered pricing based on depth and supporting materials (e.g., workbook only vs. full course with coaching). |

## Packages & Bundles

1. **Starter Package** – 4 individual sessions + 1 group session: **$900** (10% discount).
2. **Therapist Growth Bundle** – 6 supervision hours + 2 private‑practice sessions: **$1,200**.
3. **Wellness Retreat Pass** – Access to a weekend retreat (incl. yoga, somatic work, and bio‑feedback): **$1,500**.

## Discount Policies

- **Sliding Scale** – Up to 20% discount for clients with documented financial hardship (requires proof and intake form).
- **Referral Discount** – Existing clients receive a **$25** credit per referral that converts to a session.
- **Early‑Bird Launch Discount** – First 10 clients receive a **15%** discount on their first three sessions.

## Payment Terms

- Payments are collected via **HIPAA‑compliant** billing platform (e.g., SimplePractice).
- Sessions are prepaid in full; no insurance billing.
- Clients receive electronic receipts and a summary of services.

## Review & Adjustments

- Review pricing quarterly based on demand, therapist workload, and market trends.
- Adjust rates by up to 5% annually to reflect inflation and continued professional development.
