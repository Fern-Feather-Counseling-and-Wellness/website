Fern & Feather is growing

We’re Growing.

Looking for a few really good humans.

Fern & Feather Counseling and Wellness is growing, and we’re looking for thoughtful, curious, genuinely human LPCs and APCs to grow with us. We’re a therapist-owned practice in Cumming, Georgia, built around a pretty simple idea: therapy can be evidence-based, ethical, deeply human—and still happen in a place where both clients and clinicians can exhale.

We care about doing excellent clinical work. We also care about creating a work environment where therapists are trusted, supported, and allowed to be actual people.

Come practice somewhere you can be a therapist and a human.

We built Fern & Feather to be the kind of practice we would want to work in ourselves. That means thoughtful, evidence-based care without the corporate feel; professional autonomy without having to figure everything out alone; and a community of clinicians who genuinely like learning from and supporting one another.

We’re serious about ethics, good clinical care, inclusion, and continuing to grow as therapists. We’re considerably less serious about pretending we have it all together.

You might be our kind of therapist if...

You believe science and humanity belong in the same room. You’re curious, collaborative, affirming, and willing to keep learning—even when that means changing your mind.

You want autonomy and flexibility, but you also like having people down the hall who understand the weird, wonderful, occasionally exhausting work of being a therapist.

You care more about being a good therapist than looking like one.

You believe clients deserve a space where they don’t have to edit who they are in order to belong.

And you bring something of your own to the practice—a specialty, population, approach, perspective, or area of clinical curiosity that you’re genuinely excited about. We’re especially interested in clinicians who enjoy working with adults and/or adolescents.

The Nuts & Bolts

Clinicians join Fern & Feather as 1099 independent contractors with a 70/30 split—you keep 70% of collected session fees.

Build the practice that works for your life. Whether you’re looking for a small caseload alongside other work or building toward a fuller private practice, you decide what your work at Fern & Feather looks like.

Choose your own hours, the clients you want to work with, your clinical focus, your private-pay rate, and whether you see clients in person, virtually, or a mix of both. Practice within your areas of clinical interest and competence.

We provide the infrastructure, space, administrative and marketing support, and clinician community. You maintain control over your schedule, caseload, and clinical work.

We’re welcoming Georgia LPCs and APCs equally whose clinical work and values align with Fern & Feather.

For APCs: You must have and maintain independent clinical supervision outside of Fern & Feather. Supervision is not currently provided through the practice.

Our office is in Cumming, Georgia, just one mile from GA-400 at Exit 13, convenient to Alpharetta, Johns Creek, and surrounding communities.

“Work as much or as little as you want. Choose your hours. Choose your clients. Choose where you work.”
