Notice of Privacy Practices

Your Information. Your Rights. Our Responsibilities.
Effective Date: September 3, 2026

THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW HEALTH INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY.

Your Rights

You have rights regarding the protected health information (PHI) we maintain about you. Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Ask to inspect or obtain an electronic or paper copy of your health record. We will provide access or a copy as required by law and may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee when permitted.
  • Ask us to correct health information you believe is incorrect or incomplete. We may deny the request in some circumstances, but we will explain the reason in writing when required.
  • Ask us to contact you in a particular way or at a particular location. We will accommodate reasonable requests.
  • Ask us to limit what we use or share for treatment, payment, or health care operations. We are not always required to agree. If you pay for a service in full out of pocket and ask us not to disclose information about that service to your health plan for payment or health care operations, we will honor the request when required by law.
  • Ask for an accounting of certain disclosures we have made of your PHI.
  • Get a paper or electronic copy of this Notice at any time.
  • Have a person with legal authority act for you. We will verify that person's authority before treating the person as your personal representative.
  • File a privacy complaint without retaliation.

Your Choices

For certain uses and disclosures, we will ask for your written authorization or otherwise give you a choice when required by law. Examples can include sharing information with family or friends involved in your care, certain marketing communications, sale of PHI, and most uses or disclosures of psychotherapy notes if separately maintained. If you give us written authorization, you may revoke it in writing at any time, except to the extent we have already acted in reliance on it or another legal exception applies.

How We May Use & Share Your Information

We may use or disclose PHI without your written authorization when HIPAA and other applicable law allow or require it. Common examples include:

Treatment. We may use PHI to provide, coordinate, or manage your care and, when permitted, communicate with other health professionals involved in your treatment.

Payment. We may use and disclose PHI to obtain payment for services, determine benefits, or support billing and claims. When you use insurance through Headway or Alma, information necessary for billing and related operations may be handled through those organizations and your health plan.

Health care operations. We may use PHI for activities necessary to operate the practice, such as quality review, compliance, credentialing, training, business management, and certain administrative functions.

Business associates. We may use vendors that perform services involving PHI on our behalf, such as electronic health record, billing, technology, or professional-support services. When HIPAA requires it, we use written business associate agreements and require appropriate safeguards.

Required by law and public-interest purposes. We may use or disclose PHI when required or permitted by law, including certain public health activities, reports of suspected abuse, neglect, or exploitation, health oversight, workers' compensation, certain law-enforcement or judicial matters, and to avert a serious threat to health or safety when applicable legal standards are met.

Legal proceedings and requests. A subpoena, court request, or other legal demand does not automatically mean that all therapy information will be released. We will consider applicable confidentiality, privilege, authorization, court-order, and other legal requirements before disclosing information.

Mental Health Information & Psychotherapy Notes

Mental health information may receive protections in addition to HIPAA under professional ethics or other applicable law. Fern & Feather will apply any law that provides greater privacy protection when it applies.

If a clinician creates and maintains psychotherapy notes separately from the ordinary clinical record, HIPAA generally gives those notes additional protection. Progress notes and the clinical record used for treatment, payment, or health care operations are not the same as separately maintained psychotherapy notes.

Substance Use Disorder Records & 42 CFR Part 2

Certain substance use disorder (SUD) patient records are protected by the federal confidentiality law known as 42 CFR Part 2. When Fern & Feather receives, creates, or maintains information that is protected by Part 2, we will apply the protections required by Part 2 and HIPAA as applicable.

If Part 2-protected information is disclosed to a recipient that is subject to HIPAA, that recipient may generally use or redisclose the information as HIPAA permits, except that Part 2-protected information may not be used or disclosed in civil, criminal, administrative, or legislative proceedings against the patient unless the patient provides the required written consent or the use or disclosure is authorized by an appropriate Part 2 court order and accompanied by a subpoena or other legal mandate when required.

When Part 2 applies, we will follow the additional procedures required for investigations, testimony, court orders, and legal proceedings. If Fern & Feather operates a program that is itself subject to the separate Part 2 patient-notice requirements, we will provide any additional notice required for that program.

Minors & Personal Representatives

A parent, guardian, or other legally authorized person is often a minor client's personal representative for privacy purposes, but this is not true in every circumstance. Rights to consent to treatment, receive this Notice, acknowledge receipt, access records, or control disclosures may depend on Georgia law, a custody or guardianship order, the type of care involved, and other applicable law. Fern & Feather will verify legal authority when appropriate and will follow applicable law when determining who may exercise privacy rights for a minor.

Electronic Records, Communications & Technology

We use electronic systems to maintain and manage health information. Reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards are used to protect PHI. No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free.

Separate policies or consents may address electronic communication, telehealth, and optional audio transcription or AI-assisted clinical documentation. This Notice does not by itself authorize the recording or transcription of a therapy session.

Our Responsibilities

  • We are required by law to maintain the privacy and security of your PHI.
  • We will notify you as required if a breach occurs that may have compromised the privacy or security of your information.
  • We must follow the duties and privacy practices described in the Notice currently in effect and provide a copy of the Notice as required.
  • We will not use or disclose your information other than as described in this Notice unless you authorize us in writing or the law otherwise permits or requires the use or disclosure.

Changes to This Notice

We may change the terms of this Notice, and changes may apply to information we already maintain as well as information we receive in the future. When we materially revise this Notice, the current version will be available upon request, at our practice location as required, and on our website.

Questions or Complaints

If you have questions about this Notice, want to exercise a privacy right, or believe your privacy rights have been violated, contact the Privacy Officer for Fern & Feather Counseling and Wellness at info@fernandfeathercounseling.com.

You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint or exercising a privacy right.

Availability of This Notice

You may request a copy of this Notice at any time. The current Notice will be made available as required by law, including at our practice location and on www.fernandfeathercounseling.com.
